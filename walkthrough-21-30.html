If you have learned the Mana Sight ability, you can detect invisible objects and hidden treasure. In addition to disarming all enemy sabotage bombs planted throughout the fortress, there is substantial hidden treasure to uncover across all three floors.

Floor-by-Floor Bomb & Treasure Sweep:

  1. First Floor (1F): Search the lower right corner for a hidden chest containing an EMERALD (Poison Res +6, sell for gold). Head to the upper right room and examine the upper left corner for the first white sparkle — Bomb #1 Disarmed. Go left into the main hallway to find Bomb #2 Disarmed. In the upper left room, pull the crates out of the way to reach a hidden chest containing 2,000 G. Climb the stairs to 2F.
  2. Second Floor (2F): Search the room on the right for Bomb #3 Disarmed in its upper left corner. The room on the left holds a hidden chest with a PANACEA PLUS. Exit the room and walk around the northern corridor to disarm Bomb #4 Disarmed. On the far right corridor, pull the obstructing crates aside to reveal a hidden chest containing 2 MANA CRYSTALS. Climb to 3F.
  3. Third Floor (3F): In the upper left corner is Bomb #5 Disarmed (Final Bomb). Walk around to the right (do not head up the center stairs yet), and pull the crates on the left away from a chest containing a LEAF RING (HP+10 / MP+10). Now climb the stairs up into the large upper hall. Search the right wall with Mana Sight for a hidden chest containing the valuable DARKSHIELD SCROLL for Raynie. Do not teach it to Raynie just yet, or you will reset the bomb disarming objective.
  4. Sand Fortress North: Proceed out through the north exit. In the lower right corner of the outer path is a hidden chest containing another 2,000 G.

Once all five bombs are neutralized, report back to Rosch in the 1F command room. Choose to "Prioritize the Mission" to advance the plot!

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Sand Fortress Battle]

You should know your way around here very well by now. Fight all soldiers on all three floors for exp (note that enemy soldiers never spawn inside enclosed rooms). When ready, ascend to the 3F upper hall for your last stand. Transfer Raynie's Horn Charms to Stocke, swap Marco into the active party, and unequip Rosch's Wind Armlet so you retain it for later.

⚔️ Boss: Grey Shadow, Granorg Archer x2, Light Granorgite
Major Boss
Elemental Weakness: None
The Grey Shadow will always open the battle by setting up a lethal Power Zone. If any Granorg soldier or the boss occupies this tile, immediately push or assault them off of it, or their boosted strikes will wipe your party! Your top priority is eliminating Grey Shadow, which permanently dispels the Power Zone. If you have full Mana Burst gauges, execute Turn Break on Grey Shadow before he acts. In turn 1, have Marco cast Magic Boost on Stocke or Raynie. Stack elemental spells and physical strikes to quickly bring Grey Shadow down, then clean up the archers.
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [A Message from Home]

After the defensive victory and ensuing cutscenes, Stocke receives urgent dispatches from Alistel regarding the frontline war effort.

CHAPTER 22

Sand Fortress (Alternate Chapter 2)

⏳ Alternate History [SAND4] 📍 Fortress Command & Time Warp
Recommended Level Level 27
Recommended Party Stocke, Raynie, Marco
Enemies Encountered None (Peaceful Garrison)
Resting Free (2F Barracks Bed)
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Alistel's Offensive]

After receiving your destination briefing, speak with General Viola again in the command post to advance the [A Letter to Tomorrow] sidequest.

📜 Sidequest Resolution: [A Letter to Tomorrow] & Bad Ending Divergence

Use Historia to time-jump back to the "A New Mission" node in Alistel:

  1. While here in Alistel, visit the soldier in the castle right wing to teach the Darkshield Scroll to Raynie (unlocking Darkshield).
  2. Head to the Second Ward and speak to Cedric on the upper platform next to the tavern.
  3. ⚠️ Bad Ending Warning: Choosing "Don't show journal" will unlock the [A Voice Unheard] Possible History (Bad Ending).
  4. Choosing "Show Journal" successfully completes the [A Letter to Tomorrow] sidequest! Reward: NOAH AMULET (Poison/Sleep/Paralysis Res +4, sell for 5,000 G). This quest is also a strict prerequisite for the true ending epilogue "Valkyrie in Repose"!

Once finished, time-warp back to the "Alistel's Offensive" node at the Sand Fortress. Rest up in the 2F barracks bed to restore all HP/MP, climb to 3F, and take the northern exit out to the Gran Plain.

CHAPTER 23

Gran Plain (Alternate Chapter 2)

⏳ Alternate History [GRAN3] 📍 Gran Plain Frontlines
Recommended Level Level 27
Recommended Party Stocke, Raynie, Marco
Enemies Encountered Blue Granorg Archer (Sleep), Granorg Defender, Hunter (Sleep), Rose Balsam (Poison/Stone/Fire), Sabertooth Tiger, Wood Shield (Fire)
Terrain Hazard Dense Sandstorms & Ambush Patrols
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Battle of Gran Plain]

From the Sand Fortress North exit, head north one screen and west two screens. Continue marching west across the war-torn plains until you receive a scout dispatch regarding the chaotic battle conditions.

⚠️ Temporal Choice Point — Bad Ending Warning

When prompted with how to respond to the frontline crisis:
Choosing to "Go north" results in Rosch's brigade being overrun, unlocking [The Lion General] Possible History (Bad Ending).

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [A Meeting Too Late]

Return to this node (make sure you've rested at the 2F Sand Fortress bed). Choose "Look for Rosch" to locate his encircled vanguard unit in time!

⚔️ Boss: Paelzan, Blue Granorg Archer x3, Wood Shield x3
Frontline Boss
Weaknesses: Paelzan: None | Blue Archer: Sleep | Wood Shield: Fire
In Turn 1, have Marco cast Magic Boost on Raynie, Raynie cast Fire on the central Wood Shield, and Stocke cast Magic Boost on himself. In Turn 2, have Stocke use Push Assault to shove Paelzan directly into the Blue Granorg Archer behind him, then unleash two boosted Fire spells to incinerate both targets at once. Next, have Marco heal any wounded allies while Stocke and Raynie burn down the flanking Wood Shields. Finally, use Right Assault to cluster the remaining archers into a single grid tile and wipe them with area magic.
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoints: [Rosch in Crisis] & [Wolf in the Fold]

Following the battle, Rosch is gravely wounded and General Hugo's sinister political agenda begins to surface. To break through this timeline impasse, jump back to "Alistel's Offensive" and speak with Viola to unlock the [Viola's Decision] node.

📜 True Ending Prerequisite Choice

Return to the "A Meeting Too Late" node and replay the scene up to the dialogue prompt. Choose "I leave it to you." — this is a mandatory condition to unlock the true ending sidequest [At Journey's End] later in the game!

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Lives Entrusted]

With Rosch hospitalized and the battlefield stabilized, Stocke's party is recalled to the capital of Alistel.

CHAPTER 24

Alistel (Alternate Chapter 2)

⏳ Alternate History [ALST6] 📍 Alistel Castle Infiltration
Recommended Level Level 30
Party Configuration Stocke (Solo)
Enemies Encountered None (Palace Guards on alert)
Key Technique Unlocked Vanish (Invisibility)
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Homecoming]

Upon arriving in Alistel Castle, do not inspect General Hugo's office immediately! Take the central elevator down to the 1st floor first. Speak to the receptionist behind the left desk to initiate two lucrative long-term recruit sidequests:

  • [Swordsman Recruit] — Completed later in Cygnus / In Pursuit for 8,000 G.
  • [Mercenary Recruit] — Completed later in Skalla / In Pursuit for 15,000 G.

Take the elevator back up to 3F and enter Hugo's office on the left. Instead of General Hugo, you encounter a chillingly familiar figure waiting in the shadows...

⚔️ Boss: Heiss (Shadow Duel)
Solo Duel
Elemental Weakness: None
This is a 1-on-1 duel between Stocke and Heiss. Use the Change command repeatedly to manipulate the turn grid until you string together four consecutive turns for Stocke. Unleash four continuous Fire spells in a single uninterrupted combo chain to overwhelm Heiss before he can retaliate.
✨ Secret Technique Learned: Vanish & [Parting Ways] Node

For surviving the confrontation, Stocke masters the Vanish field technique! By pressing Y (or action key), Stocke turns completely invisible to enemy patrols, allowing him to sneak past heavy guard posts undetected — including the royal checkpoints at Granorg Palace.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoints: [Flight] & [Fiendish]

Head into the infirmary to consult with Sonja and arrange a clandestine escape before Hugo's death squad seals the castle exits. As you flee, Special Intelligence agents intercept you:

⚔️ Boss: Special Forces x2
Encounter
Elemental Weakness: None
One of the agents immediately establishes a Power Zone. Prioritize using Push Assault or directional attacks to keep both operatives off of the boosted tile. As long as you deny them the zone, their damage output remains negligible. Cast Fire 2–3 times on each agent to quickly dispose of them.

After the battle, Stocke collapses from exhaustion and is rescued by enigmatic beastkind travelers, carrying him away to a realm beyond human borders.

Upon waking up in the Satyros village of Celestia, leave the inn. Search the left side of the plaza with Mana Sight for a hidden chest containing a CRYSTAL CHARM (MAG+6, save for later). 📜 Sidequest Resolution: [A Flower of Promise]

Use Historia to return to "The Beginning" node in Alistel. Give the Flower Bud to the young girl tending flowers on the castle 1F. Skip ahead to the "A New Mission" node and speak to her again to complete [A Flower of Promise] → Reward: THAUMATECH ORB (MAG+5 / MDF+5). ⏳ Historia Node Checkpoints: [Unacceptable Loss], [Gauntlet], & [Beastkind's Favor]

Equipment Optimizations: Equip the Rhapsody dagger on Aht and Rune Claw on Gafka. Buy a Leather Cuirass for Gafka and a Reinforced Cape for Aht. Purchase 4 Horn Charms, 3 Fang Charms, and 5 Bishop Studs. Equip defensive accessories on Aht — while her elemental traps scale on Magic, her natural damage is already enormous, and she has low base HP. Buy 5 Sleep Wings and 5 Curse Wings from the item merchant.

Before departing, head north to the Boundary Tree and search the left wall for 2 ANTI-PARALYTICS. Head south to the village entrance and search the left side for a hidden chest with 3,000 G. Exit south toward Lazvil Hills.

Your objective is neutralizing the rampaging beast horde threatening the Celestian border. Because Aht possesses extremely high speed, she frequently acts first before turn combos can be stacked. In pre-emptive strikes, use her opening turn to plant an elemental trap on an empty tile, then use Gafka or Stocke's Assault skills to knock enemies into it! If fighting Cloudys, have Aht cast Polaris to grant party-wide immunity against poison.

Follow the woodland route east into the open clearing, head north two screens, then west and south to reach the save point. Equip Horn Charms on Stocke, transfer the magic accessories (Crystal Charm, Thaumatech Orb) to Aht, and proceed west to trigger the boss fight.

⚔️ Boss: Forest Bear, Stompy x2
Encounter
Weaknesses: Forest Bear: Ice | Stompy: Fire
Have Aht open by laying an Ice Trap. Eliminate the two Stompys immediately before they can summon additional reinforcements: a Push Assault from Gafka combined with Stocke's Fire will incinerate them. Next, shove the Forest Bear directly onto Aht's Ice Trap. Use the Change command to gather consecutive party turns, then finish the Forest Bear with two additional Ice Traps!
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Report from Vanoss]

Return to Celestia. After reporting your victory to Patriarch Barranca at the Boundary Tree, speak with Vanoss at the inn to prepare for your covert diplomatic journey into Alistel.

Upon entering Alistel, Vanoss arrives. Speak with him again to initiate the [Wandering Soul] sidequest. Then explore the city to log two more essential quests:

  • In the Second Ward, climb the two left staircases and speak to the woman to receive [Second Opinion] (leads to Raynie's Slumbering Scroll).

The Shadows will attempt to coordinate a lethal battle formation; use Stocke's or Gafka's Assault skills to shatter their grid alignment. Two of any elemental trap from Aht will kill them — knock multiple Shadows onto a single trap tile to destroy them simultaneously!

Fight the patrol soldiers on your way out of Alistel, traverse Lazvil Hills, and retrace your steps back to Celestia.

CHAPTER 28

Celestia (Alternate Chapter 3)

⏳ Alternate History [CLST2] 📍 Siege of Celestia & Time Warp
Recommended Level Level 33
Recommended Party Stocke, Raynie, Marco
Enemies Encountered None (Sanctuary under assault)
Crucial Skill Raynie Level 30 (G-Fire)

Upon arriving back in Celestia, you find Alistel shock troops and Thaumatech automaton defenses breaching the outer barrier!

⚔️ Boss: Alistel Soldier x2, Auto Defender x2
Siege Boss
Weaknesses: Alistel Soldier: Sleep | Auto Defender: Fire (Immovable)
Use two Sleep Wings immediately to lock down both Alistel Soldiers. The Auto Defenders cannot be moved or pushed. Have Marco cast Magic Boost on both Raynie and Stocke. Use Stocke's Air Assault to launch one Auto Defender into the air, followed immediately by Raynie's G-Fire to destroy it in one shot! Repeat this combo on the second Auto Defender. Finally, string together Push Assault, Air Assault, and G-Fire with the Change command to finish off the sleeping soldiers.
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Premonition of War]

Speak with Rosch at the Boundary Tree, then confer with Raul and Patriarch Barranca to decide Alistel's political succession:

  • ⚠️ Bad Ending: Choosing to "Nominate Raul" unlocks the tragic [Assassination of Raul] Possible History (Bad Ending).
  • ⚠️ Dead End: Rewinding to [Shattered Steel Arm] and choosing "Nominate Rosch" leads to an immediate roadblock due to Rosch's severed arm.
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Rosch in Agony]

To resolve this timeline roadblock, Stocke must switch over to the Standard History timeline at the "How to Disappear" node to infiltrate Granorg Palace using his new Vanish ability!

📜 Major Sidequest Resolution Sweep Across Timelines

Before crossing over to Standard History, execute this highly rewarding sidequest loop:

  1. Warp to "A New Mission" node: Climb the stairs beside the tavern in Alistel and speak to the sick man to advance [Second Opinion].
  2. Warp to "The Valkyrie" node: Ascend to the Sand Fortress mess hall and speak to the soldier on the bench to advance [Battlefield Missive] → Receive the DEATH PACT for Stocke (needed for The Blades Trial).
  3. Warp to "The Beastkind Forest" node and advance the story to "Darkness in Alistel": Speak to the boy outside the weapon shop to complete [Battlefield Missive] → Reward: CELESTIAL OIL.
  4. Head left to the Alistel tavern: The man is now saved! Speak to him to complete [Second Opinion] → Reward: SLUMBERING SCROLL (teach to Raynie immediately to unlock Sleep Break).
  5. Warp to "How to Disappear" node in Standard History to continue the main campaign.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Successful Infiltration]

Eerily enough, the palace corridors are deserted. Walk left, up the stairs, and into the Meeting Room on the right: search the right wall with Mana Sight for a hidden chest containing 2,000 G. Head back down the stairs and west two rooms to the Training Area where a save point is located. Search the lower left corner for another hidden chest containing 2,000 G. Walk up to the save point and climb the vines to trigger the ambush!

⚔️ Boss: Blue Granorg Archer x3, Granorg Defender x3
Palace Guard
Elemental Weakness: None (Archer: Low Magic DEF)
The palace garrison attempts to disrupt your turn combos — rely heavily on the Change command to assemble contiguous attack blocks. Have Marco cast Magic Boost on Raynie. Raynie can one-shot any Archer with basic magic, but requires G-Fire or an Assault assist to one-shot the heavily armored Defenders. Focus on picking off the enemy unit that sits between your party turns to secure uninterrupted combo chains. Wipe them out two by two.

After defeating the royal ambush, retreat back down through the castle basement into the Underground Passage. You can now explore the sewer halls freely. Follow the hallway north to the fork: take the left path into the Underground Library to claim a chest containing the COCKTAIL DRESS (DEF+6 / MDF+15, sell for gold). Then take the right path at the fork to make your clean escape into the city.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Princess of Light]

Stocke successfully delivers his intelligence report to Princess Eruca and regroups in the city of Granorg.

After briefing the underground resistance, speak with Will at the bar to unlock the [Sand Sword] node. While not strictly required right this second, it is an absolute requirement to save resistance lives in future chapters!

⚠️ Temporal Choice Point — Bad Ending Warning

Head east into the Downtown area. Hidden by the central tree, you will spot Princess Eruca in her cloak. When speaking with her:
Choosing "Don't push yourself" unlocks the [Princess' Charge] Possible History (Bad Ending).
Choosing "Fulfill your duty" correctly advances [The King's Daughter] sidequest!

Head east and north one screen: speak to the woman dressed in pink to initiate the [Despairing Young Man] sidequest. Then visit the Downtown armory to purchase the Soul Eater for Stocke (enormous physical ATK boost, though retain the Sorcery Epee for magic setups) and the Bloody Pike for Rosch.

📜 Rapid Sidequest Completions
  1. [The King's Daughter]: Warp to the "To Granorg" node. Speak to Anna in the upper left corner of the Second Ward in Alistel to complete the quest → Reward: RABBIT'S FOOT (MP+10 / LUC+15).
  2. [Despairing Young Man]: Warp to the "Castle Sewers" node and go to the Palace-Front Plaza. Speak to the cloaked man. Then warp back to "The Resistance" node and speak to him again to complete the quest → Reward: LEFT-CUT SCROLL (teach to Marco immediately to learn Left Cut).

With your equipment optimized and sidequests completed, exit Granorg and march across the Gran Plain toward Cornet Village!
