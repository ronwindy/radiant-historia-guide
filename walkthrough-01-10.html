 To Second Ward
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint [Daydream]

After receiving your initial briefing and the White Chronicle from Heiss, you gain full control of Stocke inside Alistel Castle.

After receiving your first mission, let's promptly ignore orders and go treasure hunting first across Alistel. To your left on 2F, you will find a chest containing 2 Soothing Balms. Take the central elevator up to the third floor and open the chest to the right which contains a Charm (ATK+1 / MAG+1 / HP+3; equip this on Raynie immediately for an early boost).

Now head down to the first floor. In the upper-right room are two chests containing a Holy Water and 2 Herbal Teas.

Proceed out to the Second Ward. The tavern is located to the left, and the equipment shop is on the right. Climb both staircases on the right and run down to open a chest containing an Herbal Tea. Return to the main corridor and head down to the First Ward.

Climb the stairs to your right and follow the perimeter path around to grab a chest with 300 G. Return to the corridor. To your left is the item shop, and the inn is on the right. Head inside the inn and climb up to the second floor to discover a chest containing Beast Skins (DEF+9 / MDF+12; equip on Stocke).

With your newly scavenged funds, return to the equipment shop in the Second Ward and purchase a Custom Sword for Stocke. Hand down his old Bronze Sword to Marco, and sell any excess equipment. As you leave the city gates, talk to Rosch, and proceed into Lazvil Hills.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint [Children of Historia]

Immediately upon your arrival, you are thrown into tutorial combat encounters. When you regain control of Stocke, head east to the Woodland Path. Below you is a chest containing 3 Soothing Balms. Continue east to trigger another narrative scene.

Once again, ignore where you are told to go and head south to open a chest containing 3 Royal Jellies. Now head north and complete another tutorial skirmish.

Proceed north to Highway West where you will unlock the Strike field ability. Use it with the attack button on roaming enemies to stun them before contact and guarantee a preemptive turn advantage! Notice the heavy crates inside the cave you pass—remember them for shortly. Proceed east to the Highway. You cannot reach the chest behind the overgrown brush yet, so cross the bridge to the east.

⚔️ Palomides & Granorg Archer x3
Scripted Battle • Unwinnable
Elemental Weakness: HA! (None)
Strategy: None. This is a scripted encounter designed to overpower your party. Stocke and his team are fatally ambushed at the bridge, triggering Stocke's subconscious resonance with the White Chronicle.

Following the fatal ambush, Stocke awakens in the cosmic realm of Historia before the twins Lippti and Teo. Upon learning of the White Chronicle's true temporal powers, you are sent back in time to the moment before the crossing.

Head south toward the roadblock. Lippti and Teo will reappear and grant you Superhuman Strength to shove heavy obstacles. Before clearing the blockade, head north back to Highway West, enter the cave, and push the crates out of the way to reach a chest containing Bronze Mail (DEF+14 / MDF+10; equip on Marco).

Return to the blockade, shove the boulders aside, defeat the pursuers, and safely escape back to Alistel.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint [Awakening]

Back in Alistel Castle's infirmary, leave the medical bay and head right to trigger a pivotal scene with Rosch.

⚠️ Major Timeline Crossroads Node: [The Beginning]

Head upstairs and speak to Rosch. You are presented with the monumental choice that defines the game's twin timelines:

  • Option 1: Join the Rosch Brigade → Initiates Alternate History (AH) under General Dias [Skip to ALST2 below].
  • Option 2: Stay under Heiss → Initiates Standard History (SH) in Specint [Skip to Chapter 06 / ALST3].

Guide Recommendation: Choose to Join the Rosch Brigade (AH) first! Doing so unlocks early sidequests and allows you to resolve an upcoming roadblock efficiently. Remember, the White Chronicle allows you to jump back to this exact decision node at any save point.

CHAPTER 03

Alistel (Alternate Prologue)

⚔️ Alternate History [ALST2] 📍 Alistel Castle
Recommended Level Level 4
Recommended Party Stocke, Raynie, Rosch
Enemies Encountered None (Recruitment Phase)
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint [Joining Rosch Brigade]

After conferring with Rosch, Lieutenant Raul, and Sergeant Kiel, Rosch officially joins your battle roster.

Because active battle parties are capped at three combatants, open your main menu and use the Party command to switch in Rosch in place of Marco. This configuration maintains Raynie's high-damage offensive magic while keeping Marco in reserve with full MP available for healing outside of combat.

Equipment Swapping: Give Marco's Bronze Mail to Raynie while Marco is in reserve. Additionally, trade Raynie's higher-damage Spear to Rosch in exchange for his Light Lance, as Rosch will be executing physical assaults far more frequently.

📜 Sidequest Unlocked [Soldier Recruit]

How to Start: Take the central elevator to 1F and speak with the receptionist behind the left desk.

Resolution: Walk down to the First Ward Inn and speak to the two wandering mercenaries resting inside. Return to the receptionist on 1F to complete the quest and claim your reward of 1,500 G!

📜 Sidequest Unlocked [Night Watch]

How to Start: In the First Ward, speak with the Alistel soldier standing on the left side of the street.

Objective: He requests 2 Anti-Sleeps to stay awake on duty. These are not sold in Alistel yet, but will become available from the merchant at the Sand Fortress (Chapter 10). Keep this quest in your log.

Financial Advice: Hold on to your 1,500 G reward rather than spending it on Alistel's basic shop inventory, as vastly superior weapons and accessories will be sold at Alma Mine and Sand Fortress very soon. Depart Alistel and head back into Lazvil Hills.

This journey through Lazvil Hills is relatively straightforward, but features two critical landmarks to note:

  1. The central bridge is destroyed, forcing the brigade to take the long detour through the southern woodland paths.
  2. Beneath the broken bridge is a collapsed cave where an Alistel soldier is trapped behind a rockfall (Marker Z). You lack explosives to rescue him right now, but keep his location in mind.

To reach Alma Mine: head west, follow the lower path south and west into the Woodland Path, go north at the fork, and exit to the west.

☠️ Critical Choice & Bad Ending Warning Choice: "Wait in ambush" vs "Send scouts"

Upon arriving at Alma Mine, you are asked how to handle the Granorg troops encamped within:

  • Choice A: "Wait in ambush" → DEAD END: [The Fallen Capital]. The Granorg vanguard bypasses your trap entirely and razes Alistel to the ground. (Complete this choice once to log the node into your White Chronicle!).
  • Choice B: "Send scouts" → Correct Path: Node [The Closed Mine]. You discover the mine entrance is sealed by massive boulders and rigged explosives.
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint [Breaking Rocks] & [The Closed Mine]

While "Send scouts" is the correct choice, the storyline reaches an unavoidable roadblock here. You need demolition expertise to breach the mine entrance without detonating the traps.

Before jumping timelines, explore the outpost:

  • Find the traveling merchant standing outside the house. Purchase 3 Horn Charms for Raynie (+10 MAG each). This massive +30 Magic stat spike will supercharge her elemental spells for multiple chapters!
  • Inside the miner's cottage, open the chest for 3 Soothing Balms.
  • Ascend the wooden ramp on the left to locate a chest containing 200 G.

Temporal Warp Instruction: Save your game at the save point, select "Go to Historia", navigate back to the [The Beginning] node, and this time speak to Rosch and tell him you are choosing to "Stay under Heiss".

CHAPTER 06

Alistel (Standard Prologue)

📜 Standard History [ALST3] 📍 Special Intelligence HQ
Recommended Level Level 6
Recommended Party Stocke (Solo)
Enemies Encountered Vlad (Specint Traitor)

Take the elevator up to the second floor of Alistel Castle to receive your covert espionage assignment directly from First Lieutenant Heiss.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint [Specint Assignment]

Descend to the First Ward item shop. Inquire with the shopkeeper to discover that your target is lurking in the Second Ward tavern.

Head up to the Second Ward and enter the tavern. Watch the tense dialogue scene unfold. When the suspicious robed operative slips out the door, trail him outside and corner him in the alley.

⚔️ Vlad (Covert Informant)
Boss Encounter • 1-on-1 Duel
Elemental Weakness: None
Strategy: If Stocke has learned Power Wave, use it for safe, high-potency ranged damage. Otherwise, execute standard physical strikes and cast Heal whenever your HP drops below 50. Vlad goes down quickly under sustained assault.
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint [Top Secret Mission]

After defeating Vlad, report back to Heiss's office on 2F. Take the elevator down to the 1F castlegate to officially commence Standard History: Chapter 1!

☠️ Critical Choice & Bad Ending Warning Choice: "Go to Alma Mine" vs "Wait for Informant"

Go west to the Highway and descend the ramp to reach the river crossing. You encounter a wounded scout with conflicting intelligence:

  • Choice A: "Go to Alma Mine" → DEAD END: [The Queen Ascendant]. Abandoning your post results in the total annihilation of the Alistel frontline and Queen Protea's total conquest. (Select once to record the node!).
  • Choice B: "Wait for Informant" → Correct Path: Nodes [The Messenger's News] & [No-Show].
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint [Echoing Hills]

Go south one screen and west toward the Woodland Path. You hear chaotic shouting echoing through the trees. Heal up and charge north to investigate.

⚔️ Desert Pirate Hertz & Red Sand Bram
Boss Encounter • Grid Synergies
Elemental Weakness: None
Strategy: Maximize your combo damage by using the Change command so Stocke, Raynie, and Marco act consecutively in a single turn cycle:
  1. Have Marco use Grapple to drag Hertz forward or into alignment.
  2. Raynie casts Thunder (boosted by 3 Horn Charms!) on the aligned target.
  3. Stocke uses Push Assault to shove Bram into Hertz's grid tile for stacking damage.
  4. In following rounds, conclude your combo chain with Stocke's Power Wave. Keep HP above 100 with items or Marco's Heal.
💣 Field Skill Acquired: Bomber Skill Node: [Struggle with Bandits]

For saving the merchant Tarquin from the bandits, he teaches Stocke the Bomber Skill!

How to use: Push explosive barrels against cracked boulders, press L to prime the fuse, step back to a safe distance, and press L again to detonate! This skill is also the master key that solves the Alma Mine blockage in Alternate History!

Let's put the new demolition skill to work across Lazvil Hills immediately:

  • Holy Water: Head east to the adjacent screen, push the barrel on the right toward the cracked rocks on the left, and detonate to claim a chest with Holy Water.
  • Breeze Armlet: Return north to the cave where the soldier was trapped. Push the barrel inside to blow the rubble. The chest yields a Breeze Armlet (SPD+1; equip on Stocke).
  • Celestial Tea: Push the barrel at the Highway East entrance down to demolish the blockade into the Southern Path. Behind the tree on the right is a chest with Celestial Tea.
📜 Sidequest Unlocked [Accident in the Cave]

Inside the cave where you retrieved the Breeze Armlet, examine the fallen soldier's body to initiate [Accident in the Cave]. (You will be able to travel back to save him later with your White Chronicle!).

Return to the Woodland Path, proceed west and south through the cleared gorge to arrive at Judgement Cliff.

Tactical Hazard Warning: The enemies inhabiting Old Spring East are manageable. However, if you venture west into Old Spring West, enemy stats scale up dramatically and falling boulders tumble from Death Mountain. Dodge the wandering monsters, grab the treasures swiftly, and retreat.

Trap Detection: Head west two screens. As you walk down the winding ledge, take slow steps and swing your sword periodically to expose and disarm invisible ground snare traps that would otherwise inflict heavy party damage.

Treasure Run:

  • At the bottom of Old Spring West, open the chest for Sniper Rounds (ATK+20 / MAG+3; vendor this for high gold!).
  • Push the nearby explosive barrel along the winding path to blow away the rocks guarding a chest with 3 Herbal Teas.
  • Enter the cave above to open a chest containing 2 Power Down Plus.

Return to the entrance at Old Spring East and take the north mountain path. Go straight north through the Giant Cave to exit directly onto the border approach of the Sand Fortress.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint [Breakthrough]

Infiltrate the Granorg-occupied fortress garrison and storm the commander's terrace on 3F to punch through to the northern border.

Field Safety: In Sand Fortress South, hug the western cliffside to bypass the hidden explosive traps planted along the main road.

Merchant Shopping Priority:

  • Buy 2 Anti-Sleeps (50 G each) to fulfill the requirement for the [Night Watch] sidequest back in Alistel.
  • Buy 6 Fang Charms (1,000 G each) → Equip 3 on Stocke and 3 on Marco for massive physical attack scaling!
  • Buy 1 Rune Spear (2,500 G) for Raynie to grant her another enormous magic boost.
  • If you have accumulated enough gold from selling excess items and defeated foes, purchase a Rune Edge (5,000 G) for Stocke and pass down his old Custom Sword to Marco.

Infiltration Strategy: While you can sprint up the central stairs straight to 3F without fighting, it is strongly recommended to engage all garrison guards. The EXP and Gold will prepare you for upcoming challenges, and Feepos here can drop the rare Vital Blade.

⚔️ Granorg Defender, Granorg Archer x2, Light Granorgite x2
Fortress Commander Boss Trial
Elemental Weakness: None (Use Magic Boost + Thunder)
Combat Execution:
  1. Turn 1: Have Marco cast Magic Boost on Raynie and Stocke.
  2. Turns 2–3: Raynie casts Thunder while Stocke uses Push Assault to eliminate the Light Granorgites and Archers in rapid succession.
  3. Boss Execution: Once the minions fall, use the Change command to string together six consecutive party turns. Alternate between Stocke's physical attacks and Raynie's high-tier spells to stagger and annihilate the high-defense Granorg Defender!

Defeating the garrison grants passage into the Sand Fortress North courtyard. The exit leading toward Gran Plain (Standard Chapter 1) lies to the northeast. Rest up back at Alistel before commencing Volume II!
