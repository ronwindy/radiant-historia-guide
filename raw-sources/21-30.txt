On the first floor, search the lower right corner for a chest which contains an

corner for a white sparkle that is your first bomb to disarm.  Go left in the

the second floor.

[Sand Fortress Battle node]
You should know your way around here pretty well by now.  I'd recommend
fighting all the enemies you can find on all three floors.  Note the soldiers
will never spawn in a room.  When you've killed enough of them to satisfy your
bloodlust, make your way up to the upper room on the third floor to make your
last stand.  I'd recommend giving Raynie's Horn Charms to Stocke, and having
Marco replace her.  You may also want to remove Rosch's Wind Armlet, since you
won't have a chance to equip it on someone else later.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Grey Shadow, Granorg Archer x2, Light Granorgite
WEAKNESS: None
STRATEGY: The Grey Shadow will always start the battle with a Power Zone.  If
anyone steps on it, absolutely get them off of it as quickly as possible or
they will kill you.  Your first priority should be to take out the Grey Shadow,
since that will clear the Power Zone.  If you have any full Mana Burst gauges,
use Turn Break on the Grey Shadow, whenever his turn comes around.  In the
first round, have Marco use Magic Boost on either Stocke or Raynie.  A couple
spells and physical attacks should finish him off.  The rest can be handled
easily without the need for further buffing or healing.
*******************************************************************************

[A Message from Home node]
After some cutscenes, you will hear about your next mission.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game

[The Resistance node]
After some more dialogue, talk to Will at the bar to unlock the [Sand Sword]
node.  While not a mandatory event for now, you will eventually have to do it.
Head east to the Downtown area.  Somewhat hidden by a tree in the center, you
will find Eruca in her cloak, and she will prompt you for a decision.  Choosing
"Don't push yourself" will unlock [Princess' Charge] ending.  Otherwise,
choosing "Fulfill your duty" will advance [The King's Daughter] sidequest.

Go east and north one screen.  Talk to the woman in pink to receive the
[Despairing Young Man] sidequest.  Head south and east one screen, and check
out the shops with new wares for sale.  I would buy a Soul Eater for Stocke and
a Bloody Pike for Rosch later.  The Soul Eater is great for Stocke's physical
attacks, but you should hold onto his Sorcery Epee for when you want him to be
casting magic instead.

Return to the "To Granorg" node.  Talk to Anna in the upper left corner of the
Second Ward to complete [The King's Daughter] sidequest and to receive the
RABBIT'S FOOT (MP+10/LUC+15).  The Luck stat improves your stealing ability, so
you may want to hold onto it for later, but it's not critical at all.

Return to the "Castle Sewers" node and head up to the Palace-Front Plaza.  Talk
to the man in the cloak to advance the [Despairing Young Man] sidequest.
Return to "The Resistance" node and talk to him again to complete the
[Despairing Young Man] sidequest and receive the LEFT-CUT SCROLL for Marco,
which you should teach to him immediately.

Leave Granorg and head to the Gran Plain to reach Cornet Village.
