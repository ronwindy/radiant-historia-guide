I. INTRODUCTION

This is a complete walkthrough for Radiant Historia, one of the best RPGs that
I have played in years.  If you want to play an RPG with awesome music, an epic
plot, and simple, yet engaging battle mechanics, this is the game for you.

I hope this walkthrough will help you have more fun with the game and provide
information organized as efficiently as possible.  It includes the elemental
weaknesses of every monster, as well as every treasure in the game that I'm
aware of.  The recommended levels are for the main character and based on
fighting every random encounter, so as to avoid the need to level-grind.
However, even if your levels are well below mine, don't worry about it.

I also avoid farming of gold or equipment of any kind during the walkthrough.
The only exception is for stealing equipment, since it's guaranteed that the
enemy will have the item.  I am well aware that there are some enemies that
drop "ultimate" equipment and that bosses with such equipment can be fought
repeatedly to get more of it.  However, it takes too much time for not enough
reward, in my opinion.

I will do my best to avoid spoilers to the plot in the walkthrough.  That said,
I'll obviously have to mention character names, dungeon names, and when you 
are told to do something or go somewhere.  If that is more information than you
want to know about the plot, then you may want to try the GameFAQs message 
boards.  Be warned that the Node Completion section does contain spoilers.

If you have some new information, know something I missed, know a better way to
do something, got a death threat, or a marriage proposal, please send your 
comments to the aforementioned email address.
