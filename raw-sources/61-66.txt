him with Aht at the beginning, equip her with Raynie's Thauma Charms, and equip

*******************************************************************************

WEAKNESS: Lightning

[Turning of the Tide node]
After the plot takes you out of the palace and waterway, you have to make your
way to the Castle Gates.  On your way there, you will encounter some Alistel
Officers.  Make sure to have Stocke steal two items from them, the last of
which will be the Glaux Dagger (ATK+31/MAG+85/HP-30, equip on Aht).  There are
stationary Cannons on the map that have no items to steal.  However, if you
encounter a Cannon in a battle where you originally saw another enemy on the
map, steal a Freikugel Zamiel (ATK+18/MAG+75/DEF-12, equip on Eruca) from them
for a huge magic boost!  Rest up at the inn, bring in Aht to replace Gafka, and
continue east to run into an old friend.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Dias Guard x2, Gold, Sapphire
WEAKNESS: None, Ice, Fire
STRATEGY: Lay two Ice Traps behind Gold, and Push Assault him onto it.  Take
out the Dias Guards with Burst Light and any Star Trap.  Then, steal twice from
Sapphire to get the Beast Claw (ATK+80/HP-50, equip on Gafka).  Finally, use a
Fire Star Trap to finish off Sapphire.
*******************************************************************************

If Eruca hasn't reached Level 60 yet to learn her final spell, I'd recommend
fighting some of the enemies below to do so.  Afterwards, I would use Stocke,
Gafka, and probably Rosch could use the experience the most.  Equip Rosch with
the Diamond Mail from earlier.  Head up to meet the final line of defense.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Auto Defender x2, Viola
WEAKNESS: None, Poison
STRATEGY: This fight can be pretty hard without Gafka.  Viola's uses Divine
Protection, which negates the next 5 hits against her.  Luckily, she is sitting
right at the center of the battlefield.  Musou will not only completely break
down her Divine Protection, but also deal some good damage on its last hit.
Use the Change command to string together five consecutive turns: two of Gafka
and three of Stocke.  Use two Musous on Viola and three Will o' Wisps to defeat
them all quickly.
*******************************************************************************

[Viola's Last Stand node]
After a brief respite, head up to the throne room and talk to Eruca, where the
scene will shift back to Alistel.

The path through this area is very straight-forward.  Head up to an
intersection and right to a chest which contains 2 SUPERIOR TEAS.  Then, walk
up to the teleporter to the next floor.

Head right to an intersection and take the lower right path to a chest which
contains a DIVINE WATER.  Return to the intersection and follow the upper right
path to the teleporter to the next floor.

Go down to an intersection and search the area to the right for a hidden chest
which contains the MANA CAPE (DEF+21/MDF+68/SPD-3, sell).  From the
intersection, proceed down to the teleporter to the next floor.

Walk left to a fork and search the upper area for a hidden chest which contains
the WIND GOD BOOK for Gafka.  From the fork, head left to an intersection and
take the lower left path.  Pull the barricade out of the way to access a chest
which contains a MYTHRIL MAIL (DEF+38/MDF+64/SPD-2, sell).  Return to the
intersection and continue left to the teleporter to the next floor.

Proceed up to an intersection and search the right area for a hidden floor
panel.  Step on the switch to activate the next teleporter.  From the
intersection, head up, but hug the left wall until you can go through a path
that is rather hard to see.  Equip Aht with Thauma Charms and examine the
thaumachine there.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Special Thaumachine
WEAKNESS: Lightning
STRATEGY: Lay a Volt Star Trap behind him and Push Assault him onto it.
*******************************************************************************

For defeating it, you will receive the BLAST CORE for Rosch and advance the
[Thaumatech's End] sidequest.  Sadly, there is never a better time to turn in
the quest than now, so I'd just recommend getting it out of the way.  Walk all
the way out of the lab and talk to the old man by the entrance to complete the
[Thaumatech's End] sidequest and receive a MEDIBRANCH.  It wouldn't hurt to
rest up at the inn, as long as you're here.  When you're ready, return to the
Underground Lab B5 and take the upper teleporter to the next floor.

Head right to an intersection.  Above and below you are two barrels that you
will need to pull to blow away the blockade to the right.  Once you've gotten
past them, continue right to the teleporter to the next floor.

Hug the left wall until you can go through another hard to see path.  Talk to
the engineer to receive the [A Scholar's Creed] sidequest.  From the fork, go
down and search the right path for a hidden barrel.  Pull it around to the main
path to blow away the machine blocking the teleporter to the next floor.

Go left to the intersection and down to a hidden chest which contains a
CELESTIAL DEW.  Take the lower left path to find a chest which contains
2 TOURNIQUETS.  Finally, head down the lower right path to find a hidden floor
panel.  Push the switch to activate the teleporter to the next floor up the
center path.

Along the lower left and lower right paths are two hidden barrels.  However,
there are four barricades to use them on.  First, use them to blow up the right
barricades to gain access to a chest which contains a MEDIBRANCH.  Teleport to
the previous floor and back to make the barrels respawn.  This time, use them
to blow away the left barricades.  Search the area beyond to find a hidden
floor panel.  Push the switch to activate the teleporter to the next floor up
the center path.

Walk up and talk to Hugo to be prompted for a decision.  Choosing to
"Take the power" will unlock the [Hugo the Omnipotent] ending.

[Demigod node]
Return to this node, and instead choose to "Draw your sword."  You'll fight
Hugo, but it will be pitifully easy.  Use whatever you want.

[Wielder of Infinity node]
Talk to Fennel and pick up the OLD MAGIC BLADE above the save point.  Before
restoring the Historica, return to "The Beginning" node.  Talk to Fennel in the
lab next door to complete the [A Scholar's Creed] sidequest and receive the
HASTE CORE for Rosch.

Return to the "Celestian War" node.  Talk to Sonja to install those new Core
into Rosch's Gauntlet and learn those new abilities.  Then, talk to Elm to
restore the power of HISTORICA (ATK+58/MAG+28/SPD+2) and unlock the
[Blade's Resurrection] node].  I prefer having a better magic stat for Stocke,
but hold onto the sword for later. You can also return to "Gafka & the Scrolls"
to have Bergas teach that new scroll to Gafka too.

Return to the "Demigod" node.  I would bring in Aht and Gafka for this round.
Talk to Hugo for a slightly more challenging battle.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Hugo
WEAKNESS: None
STRATEGY: Hugo can summon two Alistel Officers, but they're nothing to worry
about.  Have Aht lay any Star Trap behind Hugo, then use Push Assault and Right
Assault on him.  He'll probably summon those officers now.  Lay any Star Trap
at the center of grid.  Then, use your new Wind God Strike to drag everyone
onto the trap and a Right Assault to finish off Hugo.  You could steal a
Venus Ring (HP+70/MP+50) from him and repeat the fight to get an unlimited
supply, but I don't like HP/MP boosting rings.
*******************************************************************************

[An End to Madness node]
After defeating Hugo, it appears that we have finally brought peace to Alistel.
OR DID WE?!

BOSS TIME: Master x4
WEAKNESS: This is the hardest boss fight in the game.  They can inflict Sleep
on your party members, Power Down, and deal massive damage with their spells.
Having 9 Sky Drops is a must to resist sleep.  You pretty much need Eruca for
Divine Light, in order to survive.  Always play on the defensive.  Your first
priority should be to establish Divine Light on everyone, using Marco's
Trans-turn to help out.  Make sure to keep track of how many hits each
characters' shield has left on it.  If the guys have at least one hit left,
they should be fine.  However, with her low HP, Eruca needs to have a full
Divine Light shield at all times.  You could use Star Traps with Aht to take
them out one at a time.  However, I think it's faster to hit them all at once

automatic, assuming you've completed all the sidequests up to now.

between the two Silent Soul, and Stocke use Push Assault on Garland.  Then, use
Cross Star and two Heat Blades on Garland, which will take out the two Silent
Souls.  Dancing Death and a G-Fire should finish off Garland.
