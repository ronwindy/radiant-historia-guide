Recommended Level: 42
Recommended Party: Stocke, Raynie, Marco
Enemies: None
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
[Scorching Battlefield node]
After resting at the inn, head west to the plaza, and look for Ricky along the
main road.  Talk to him to receive the [An Unwanted Reunion] sidequest.  Head
to the tavern and talk to the green-haired girl, Mimel, twice.  Return to the
"Chi-Clad Warrior" node and talk to the bartender.  Return to the "Scorching
Battlefield" node and talk to Mimel again to be prompted for a decision.
Choosing to "Capture Mimel" will unlock the [Simmering Fury] ending.

Otherwise, choosing to "Let Mimel go" will advance [An Unwanted Reunion].  Talk
to Ricky again to complete the [An Unwanted Reunion] sidequest and receive the
SABLE LARME (DEF+33/MDF+25/MAG+5, equip on Raynie).

Head up to Cygnus HQ and talk to Garland to be prompted for a decision.
Choosing to "Stay in Cygnus" will unlock the [A Mighty King's Death] ending.

[Warlord's Request node]
Instead, return to this node and choose to "Lead the way".  After some events,
you will receive the BLOODIED ARMLET.

[Dias Strikes node]
Return to the "Desert City Messiah" node and proceed with events as before.
This time, you will get an extra scene that will unlock the [Hedge in Exile]
node.  Return to the "Warlord's Request" node, choose to "Lead the way" and
events will proceed more favorably this time.

[Stocke Strikes node]
To the left is a traveling merchant with new accessories.  Buy 8 Crystal Charms
for Stocke, Raynie, & Eruca.  If you have the money, buy 4 Iron Charms for
Rosch & Gafka later.  Stocke should pretty much be a full-time mage for the
remainder of the game, since his physical attacks don't deal as much damage.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game
Stocke: Pieti Edge, Gospel Armor, Crystal Charm x3
Raynie: Magic Spear, Sable Larme, Crystal Charm x3
Marco: Madre Blade, Iron Mail, Bishop Stud x3
Aht: Silver Dagger, Life Robe, Bishop Stud x3
Eruca: Solid Slugs, Rune Dress, Crystal Charm x3
Extra: Moirae Manteau, 4 Iron Charms

[Secret Weapons node]
Go north to begin taking out the Ballistas.  Head right to find a chest which
contains 3 SPECIAL TEAS.  The other paths are blocked for now, so head west to
find your first batch of ballistas.  Let's take out the one in the upper left
corner first.  Then, the one in the lower left corner.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Ballista, Granorg Blitzer x3
WEAKNESS: Fire, Sleep
STRATEGY: You can use Sleep Gas to crowd control the Blitzers.  If you have
enough starting turns, use two Left Assaults and a G-Frost to take them out.
Use the Change command to string together all 10 turns.  Have Marco use all of
his turns casting Trans-turn on Stocke.  Then, arrange your turns like so:
Double Slash, Mana Shot, Double Slash, Mana Shot, Double Slash, Mana Shot,
Steal, Steal, Steal, Steal, to boost the combo bonus, hopefully allowing you to
steal the FREIKUGEL KREUZ (ATK+4/MAG+35/DEF-3) for Eruca.  Equipping him with
the Gospel Armor to boost his Luck will help immensely.
*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Ballista x2, Red Granorg Archer
WEAKNESS: Fire, None
STRATEGY: Have Stocke cast Heat Blade to take out both Ballistas.  Then, use a
Left Assault and G-Frost to defeat the Archers.
*******************************************************************************

You can equip Stocke with his Moirae Manteau and hand down the Gospel Armor to
Marco after stealing the new gun for Eruca.  Before heading back, check out the
well in the area to the west.  Climb down the well to find a chest which
contains a CELESTIAL OIL.  The man here appears to have been waiting for you,
but won't say why.  Remember that for later.  Return back two screens.

[Garland's Arrival node]
Garland will arrive with explosives.  Unfortunately, they do not work on the
northern barricades, so drag a barrel to blow up the eastern rocks.  Go east
two screens and search the upper right corner for a hidden chest which contains
3000 G.  Head back west, then north, and west a screen to find a chest which
contains an IRON CHARM.  Return east and head north.

[Destroy the Ballistas node]
Search along the upper wall to find a hidden chest which contains the
POISONER'S SCROLL for Raynie.  Then, take out the two ballista in the upper
right and lower right corners, where you can apply the same strategies as the
before.  Temporarily give Raynie's Crystal Charms to Aht, bring her in to
replace Marco, and head back to talk to Garland.

[Terrain Advantage node]
With the ballistas destroyed, there's only one more enemy to take care of, as
well as an old friend.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Gardner, Palomides
WEAKNESS: Lightning
STRATEGY: Use a Magic Herb on Aht and have Eruca cast Regenerate.  Gardner can
cast support and healing spells, so you want to take him out first.  I would
avoid using the Change command to set up combos, as their attacks can really
hurt.  Your main source of damage will be Electric Traps, but don't worry so
much about getting both of them onto the same one, as taking out Gardner first.
You could steal a Giant Armor from Palomides, but you already have better.
*******************************************************************************

[A New Journey node]
After returning to Cygnus, leave the city to proceed to Chapter 6.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game

Marco: Madre Blade, Gospel Armor, Bishop Stud x3

[Waiting at Skalla node]
Before heading to Skalla, we've got a lot of sidequests to do.  First, return
to Cygnus and talk to Garland to receive the [Moon Armlet] sidequest.  Then,
head over to the equipment shops.  Talk to Hertz & Bram over there to receive
[The Good Thief's Way] sidequest.  Go back to the Itolia Wasteland.

At this point, Aht should have learned Cross Star and Eruca should have learned
Blizzard.  If so, you really shouldn't have to worry about using traps for now.
Just use Assault and those two spells to deal with all the enemies.

Return to the well that is north, west, and north one screen from the initial
save point.  Go down the well and the man, named Sword, is ready to talk to you
now and give you [The Living Legend] sidequest.  Leave the well area, then walk
east two screens and north two screens.  You should see a small rock formation
in the middle.  Search around there for the hidden MOON ARMLET.

Head west to find some rocks you'll need to blow away.  Go past them, and west
again.  You can find a hidden barrel along the lower cliff that you will need
in addition to the two visible ones, in order to get past the rocks to the
north, but if you do, you'll be well rewarded with a chest which contains the
TUNGSTEN MAIL (DEF+42/MDF+24/SPD-2, equip on Marco).  Save the Gospel Armor for
later.  Talk to Garland at Cygnus to complete the [MOON ARMLET] sidequest and
receive the SUNA-WATARI (ATK+42/SPD-4, sell).

Return to "The Beginning" node.  Talk to Wand, the boy at the weapon shop, and
choose "Hope" to advance [The Living Legend] sidequest.  While you are here,
talk to the child by the tavern to advance [The Good Thief's Way] sidequest.

Return to "The Messenger's News" node and search the upper right corner for the
hidden SHADE PACT for Stocke.  Return to the "Castle Sewers" node.  Talk to a
man in the lower left corner named Pentacle, and choose "The Past" to advance
[The Living Legend] sidequest.  While you are here, talk to the boy to the left
of the inn to advance [The Good Thief's Way] sidequest.

Return to the "How to Disappear" node, go down the sewers, and head west one
screen.  Search the left of the upper paths to find the hidden SUNLIGHT PACT
for Eruca.

Return to "The Beastkind Forest" node and talk to Chalice, the lady by the
equipment shop.  Choose "All Things" to advance [The Living Legend] sidequest.
Search to the left of the sparkling bushes at the center of town to find the
hidden METEORITE PACT for Aht.

Return to the "Waiting at Skalla" node and talk to Sword at the well to
complete [The Living Legend] sidequest.  He will introduce you to Vainqueur,
who can teach new abilities to Stocke, Aht, and Eruca.  Talk to him again to
receive [The Blades Trial], [The Starlit Trial], [The Light Trial], and
[The Three Contracts] sidequests.  If you've followed the walkthrough up to
now, you should be able to teach 3 abilities to Stocke, 2 abilities to Aht, and
3 abilities to Eruca.

Go back to Cygnus and talk to Bram to complete [The Good Thief's Way] and
receive the PIERCING SCROLL from Hertz for Raynie.  This would also be a good
time to return to "A New Mission" to teach those new scrolls to Raynie.

When you are ready, return to the Itolia Wasteland.  Simply head straight north
and blow up all the rocks along the way to reach the northern exit that will
lead you to Skalla.

Many enemies here are weak to fire, so Stocke's new Wisp Blade can be very
useful in the right battles.  Eruca's Burst Light can also be quite obscene.
At the fork, head south, then east two screens where you will bump into Liese.
Talk to her to advance the [Mankind & Beastkind] sidequest.

Return to the entrance and follow the northern path to the next screen until
you reach an open area.  In the lower left corner is a chest which contains a
BEAST CHARM (ATK+10) that you should save for later.  Examine the corpse in the
upper right corner to advance the [Mankind & Beastkind] sidequest.  Open the
hidden chest next to him which contains the ROLL-CUT SCROLL for Marco.  Return
to Liese once more to advance the [Mankind & Beastkind] sidequest.

I would strongly recommend leaving and completing some sidequests at this time,
since the rewards are really good, and the next couple areas are a little
tough.  Return to "A New Mission" to teach that new scroll to Marco and to
"The Three Contracts" to teach the new Pact to Aht.  Return to the
"Echoing Hills" node and defeat the bandits again.  Talk to Tarquin to advance
the [Mercenary Recruit] sidequest.

Return to the "Castle Sewers" node and talk to Liese in the northwest corner of
the Downtown area to be prompted for a decision.  Choosing to "Leave it to her"
will unlock the [Chains of Hatred] ending.  Instead, return to the decision
point and choose to "Believe in him" to advance the [Mankind & Beastkind]
sidequest.  Talk to the soldier by the save point on Main Street to complete
the [Mankind & Beastkind] sidequest and receive the PROMISED KNIFE
(ATK+27/MAG+75/HP-30, equip on Aht).  Aht should now be able to one shot almost
everything with her Cross Star spell.

Return to "The Resistance" node and talk to Will, giving him the Sand Sword and
receiving 1500 G.  Like with the Escort event earlier, you don't have to do it
now, but if you don't, you will have to do it sooner or later anyway.  This
will also enable you to unlock two rather obscure nodes, as well.

Continue the plot to the "Capital in Flames" node at Granorg.  Walk north until
you automatically talk to Will to unlock the [Will's Sword] node.  Return to
the "Wounded Otto" node and continue the plot through the Sand Fortress to
unlock the [Will to the Rescue] node.

Return to the "Waiting at Skalla" node and proceed to Skalla, as before.  Talk
to Tarquin to advance the [Mercenary Recruit] sidequest.  Proceed with events
as before until you make it back to the Abyssia Forest.

Follow the lower path east one screen to a fork.  If you like, head east to see
how Liese's relationship is going.  Otherwise, head north.  Follow the left
path to the next area where there is a chest which contains a HEALING FRUIT.
Go back to the fork and take the right path to the next area.

There is another fork ahead.  After clearing the enemies, search behind the
trees on the lower left side for a barrel.  Move it down to the rocks along the
lower path to gain access to another small area.  Search around for a hidden
chest which contains the GAIA BLADE (ATK+30/MAG+25/HP+100, equip on Marco).

Continue east to a long road of tall grass.  At the end of the path is a dead
end with a chest which contains 2 TORNIQUETS.  Return to the last fork and
follow the northern path to the ruins.  You should rest up at Skalla before
entering, first.
