Even though she's a bit of a liability at this point, replace Raynie with Aht,

trap.  Since she doesn't have any assaults right now, and she's rather frail,
it's more trouble than it's worth right now, but I'd still recommend using her
for the experience.  Against Goblins, it may not be a bad idea to have her cast
Polaris in a pre-emptive strike, to protect against poison.

back there now, then skip to SAND3, but I wouldn't do that.  Also, there is a
lot of hidden treasure in previous areas that we could get now.  Again, I will
wait until it is more convenient to do so.

[Preparing a Meal node]
Go west and cut through the tall grass again.  In the upper right corner of the
area, you will find a WITHERED BRANCH.  Head east to the central Gran Plan area
and north to find another WITHERED BRANCH.  Proceed north to the Gran Plain
Northwest area, and hug the east wall until you find yet another WITHERED
BRANCH.  Follow Aht's direction, and walk south two screens to the South Hill.

At this time, I would recommend switching Raynie in to replace Aht, as well as
giving her back the Horn Charms.  Search by the tree on the right to find the
last WITHERED BRANCH and something else will find you.

WEAKNESSS: Poison, Fire
STRATEGY: FINALLY!  A boss fight with elemental weaknesses we can exploit!
Start with Marco's Push Assault on Fumble, Fire from Stocke to also take out
the mushroom, and Poison Break on Fumble.  In the next round, have Marco use
Magic Boost on Raynie, Stocke uses Right Assault on the mushroom, and Raynie
casts Fire on the two.  Once they're all out of the way, I would use the Change
command to string together six consecutive turns of alternating between
physical attacks and spells to finish off the Fumble before he can call for
more mushroom backup.

Forest Clearing and talk to Vanoss or Liese to sleep the night there.  Aht will
also give Stocke the White Page.  In the morning, use the save point to return
to Historia.  With the White Page, Lippti and Teo will unlock your first Mana
Burst: Turn Break, which can delete an enemy's turn panel.  You can fill up the
Mana Burst gauge by either dealing or receiving damage.

to return to the Gran Plain Northwest area.  Continue north for a small scene.
