GRANORG (Standard Chapter 3) [GRNG4]

Recommended Party: Stocke, Raynie, Marco

=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
[Capital in Flames node]
Head north to where you will find Will, who tells you where Eruca is.

[Eruca's Whereabouts node]
Head west past the inn to find some old friends of yours.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Granorg Defender x4
WEAKNESS: None
STRATEGY: These guys are so easy, I wouldn't even bother with Magic Boost.  Use
Push Assault on the middle Defender, Air Assault, and G-Fire to take out the
first two.  Defeat the other two in a similar fashion.
*******************************************************************************

[Aht's Fight node]
Aht will give you the BLACK PAGE and rejoin the party.  Head west to the tavern
where you will find Eruca and she will join the party.  At this point, I
recommend using Aht and Eruca, arranging your equipment as follows.  Despite
being a magic-heavy party, they're pretty behind on levels, and Eruca gets the
least screen time of all the characters to compensate for that.  Eruca at least
has Trans-turn to help you get an extra assault in there.  Meteora and Holy
Arrow can also be an effective combination.  You should also visit Historia
with the Black Page to unlock your next Mana Burst.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game
Stocke: Soul Eater, Runic Mail, Fang Charm x3
Raynie: Magic Spear, Runic Mail, Horn Charm x3
Marco: Wisp Blade, Runic Mail, Bishop Stud x3
Aht: Silver Dagger, Reinforced Cape, Bishop Stud x3
Eruca: Brass Shell, Rune Dress, Crystal Charm, Thaumatech Orb, Horn Charm
Extra: Sorcery Epee, Bloody Pike, Runic Mail, Horn Charm x3

Leave the tavern.  If you saved Claire in the sewer earlier, you will bypass
the next event and unlock the [Something Precious] node.  Otherwise, you'll
have to fight your way out.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Blue Granorg Archer x3, Granorg Defender, Hermit Pierre
WEAKNESS: Sleep, None, None
STRATEGY: If you have Raynie or Marco in your party, you can use Sleep Break or
Sleep Gas on the Archers to take them out of the action.  Otherwise, use Trans-
turn, a couple Right Assaults, and a Sleep Wing to do so.  Have Aht finish off
the lowest, weakest Archer with her Attack, and possibly heal, if necessary.
Use the Change command to have Aht lay any Trap there and set up three
consecutive turns of Stock or Eruca casting Trans-turn on him.  Use two Left
Assaults and a Push Assault to kill the Defenders.  Again, use the Change
command to string together four consecutive turns to use two Traps and Assaults
on Pierre to defeat him.  You can then use the Change command to your heart's
content to defeat the remaining Archers.
*******************************************************************************

[Pierre's Betrayal node]
[Rescue the Princess node]
Leave the same way you got in here.  I would strongly recommend fighting the
enemies on your way out again, so that Aht & Eruca can gain some much needed
levels, even at the Commercial District to the east.  At the Castle Gates, talk
to the man in the cloak to receive the [Assassination of Dias] sidequest.  We
could complete a bunch of sidequests right now, but none of them have great
rewards, so I'll just save them for when it is more convenient.  Continue down
to leave Granorg and head for the Sand Fortress.

38) Lazvil Hills (Alternate Chapter 4) [LZVL6]
==============================================================================

LAZVIL HILLS (Alternate Chapter 4) [LZVL6]
Recommended Level: 40
Recommended Party: Stocke, Gafka, Rosch
Enemies: Alistel Grunt (Sleep), Alistel Soldier, Iron Shield (Fire)
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
This is where the difficulty really spikes in the game.  If you have not been
fighting every enemy up to now, you're probably underleveled.  Still, buying a
ton of those Sleep Wings will help immensely.  I recommend using Gafka and
Rosch, because they're so far behind on levels, since we don't have access to
them in Standard History.  At the very least, give Rosch some time to gain
experience in battle.

[Winds of War node]
The path is very straight-forward here.  Make sure that Rosch has a full Mana
Burst gauge before wrapping things up.  Head north two screens, bring Aht in to
replace Gafka, and proceed to find your mark.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Clockwork Thunder x4, Thaumachine
WEAKNESS: Lightning
STRATEGY: The Clockwork Thunders have little HP, but will explode for massive
damage if they ever get a turn.  Rosch should lead with his Seyfried Mana Burst
to destroy them first.  Then, simply use Electric Traps and assaults to deal
with the Thaumachine.  He will continually summon more Clockwork Thunders, so
make sure to have Rosch use Gull Swing to take them out.
*******************************************************************************

[Call to Destruction node]
Back at Celestia, you may want to remove Rosch's Rose Ring, as you won't have
an opportunity to do so later.  Talk to Raul and you will be prompted for a
decision.  Choosing to "Join with someone else" and "Team up with Granorg" will
unlock [The Eternal War] ending.

[A New Alliance node]
Instead, return to this node and proceed to the same decision.  This time,
choose to "Team up with Forgia" to proceed with the plot.  Head up to the
Boundary Tree and talk to Gafka about your options.

[Gafka's Past node]
Unfortunately, there's nothing you can do for now, as you cannot have obtained
the Beast Mark.  Return to the "Death of a Friend" node in Standard History.

STRATEGY: Use Push Assault to mitigate his damage.  You should be able to use

Afterwards, you will be treated to the inn, where you should rest up.  Check

and search to the right for a hidden chest which contains 2000 G.  West of the

Return east and follow the path north.  Before entering the tavern, search

WEAKNESS: Beer
STRATEGY: With Aht back, the strategy is simple.  First, use a Magic Herb on
Aht.  Then, have her lay any elemental trap behind him and Push Assault him
onto it.  Continue laying traps and using the appropriate Assault until you
defeat him.  You shouldn't even need to bother healing.
*******************************************************************************

[Saving Aht node]

[Inquiry in the Desert node]
Rest up at the inn, then return to Cygnus HQ to the west and north.  Talk to
the man by the left torch twice and choose "I got it" to learn what you need to
do next.  Go south to the plaza.  In the lower right area, there are to men
looking at each other, and a new man above them, a mercenary.  Talk to the
mercenary to join the tournament.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Blue Sniper, Blue Soldier, Lizard Rider, Red Sniper, Red Soldier
WEAKNESS: Lightning, Lightning, None, Ice, Ice
STRATEGY: Use a Magic Herb on Aht and have her lay an Electric Trap in the
middle back row.  Then, have Aht use Push Assault on the Blue Soldier and
Stocke use Right Assault on him to defeat those two.  Have Aht lay an Ice Trap
in the same spot while Stocke heals up.  Then, have Aht use Push Assault on the
Res Soldier and Stocke use Left Assault to defeat those to.  Finally, lay any
trap in the same spot and Push Assault to defeat the Lizard Rider.

[The Tournament node]

*******************************************************************************

WEAKNESS: None

back row, using Traps and the appropriate Assaults to win the round.
*******************************************************************************

For winning the second round, you will receive the MADRE BLADE (ATK+24/MAG+19/
HP+75, equip on Stocke).  Rest up at the inn and talk to the mercenary again
for round three, when you'll be prompted for a decision.  Choosing to
"Go with Aht's plan" will unlock the [Aht's Sacrifice] ending.

[Chi-Clad Warrior node]

[Breaker of Chi node]

to Gafka.  He will teach Stocke the power of Chi, which will unlock the
[A Beastkind Warrior] node.  Return to the "Chi-Clad Warrior" node, choose to
"Stop Aht", and you will get a different result this time.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Gold Shell Gadeff
WEAKNESS: Poison
STRATEGY: Have Aht lay a Poison Trap behind him and Stocke use Push Assault.
Then, use a Magic Herb on Aht.  Keep him in the back row, using any elemental
Traps and the appropriate Assaults to defeat him.
*******************************************************************************

[Search for Friends node]
[A New Battle node]
For defeating Gadeff, you will have won the tournament and the GOSPEL ARMOR
(DEF+33/MDF+23/LUC+20, equip on Stocke).  You can and are intended to continue
with the plot at this point.  However, there are a couple optional rounds to
the tournament in which you can participate.  These are end-game difficult
fights, but with the right preparations, you can do so now.

First, rest up at the inn.  Buy 10 Tourniquets at the item shop.  Then, buy
3 Bishop Studs and equip them on Stocke.  Finally, make sure he has the Madre
Blade and Aht has the Life Robe.  Both characters should also have maxed out
their Mana Burst gauge.  Talk to Gadeff to receive [The Next Foe] sidequest and
prepare to face it.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Crush Bear
WEAKNESS: Ice
STRATEGY: Use two Sleep Wings on him and hope one of them sticks.  If you die
or sleep doesn't stick in the first round, just reset.  Once you do, use a
Magic Herb on Aht, and use the Change command on him until you get 10
consecutive turns.  Place two Ice Traps behind him, Push Assault, two Ice Traps
below him, Left Assault, and finish him off with Fire spells.
*******************************************************************************

For winning the fourth round, you will complete [The Next Foe] sidequest and
win the PIETI EDGE (ATK+30/MAG+48/SPD-2), which you will definitely want to
save for Stocke later.  Rest up at the inn, buy up to 30 Holy Waters, and make
sure to have a Poison Wing.  Talk to Gadeff to receive [The Road to Victory]
sidequest and prepare for your final foe.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Mist Spider
WEAKNESS: None
STRATEGY: There are two keys to winning this battle: survival and damage.  This
time, you won't be able to rely on Sleep and he will be able to kill either
character in one shot, but never both at once, fortunately.  If Stocke dies,
use a Holy Water, or if Aht dies, cast Resurrection.  Always play it
conservatively until he either tries to use Fear or Speed Break on you.  To
kill him, you will need to rely on Curse and Poison Wings.  Use them on him
until you inflict them both, which you can tell when you see two damage numbers
after he attacks.  Afterwards, if you have a spare turn, only attack him,
saving your MP for Resurrection.  Once his HP bar looks like it's depleted,
attack him for the win.
*******************************************************************************

For winning the final round, you will complete [The Road to Victory] sidequest
and receive the MOIRAE MANTEAU (DEF+48/MDF+41, equip on Stocke).  Equip Stocke
with the Pieti Edge too, and save his Gospel Armor for later.  Talk to Gadeff
to advance the [Swordsman Recuit] sidequest.  This can only be done after
completing "The Next Foe" and "The Road to Victory" sidequests.

Rest up at the inn and return to Cygnus HQ.  Talk to the man by the left torch
