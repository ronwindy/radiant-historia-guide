III. Node Completion List

The following is a complete list of how to unlock all 236 nodes in the game.
They are sorted by chapter to make it easier to figure out which one(s) you are
missing.  This is probably best viewed when you are at the end of the game, as
many of the node titles and their requirements contain extreme spoilers to the
plot, and it would be difficult to sift through the entire main walkthrough for
a handful of nodes.  Furthermore, while you may get some sidequests very early
in the game, they may not be completable until much later in the game.
Finally, I will assume roadblocks are automatically resolved during the plot,
as in that case, it's simply easier to look up that section in the walkthrough.

050 - Marco the Calm (sidequest)
********************
During the "A New Mission" node at Alistel, on the right side of the first
floor are two doors.  Enter the left door and talk to the blue soldier to
receive this sidequest.  You need to find four scrolls for Marco.

1) Right-Cut Scroll: Reward from "Accident in the Cave" sidequest (see 036).
2) Left-Cut Scroll: Reward from "Despairing Young Man" sidequest (see 103).
3) Sleep Gas Scroll: During "To Cornet Village" at Cornet, Standard Chapter 3,
(see CRNT1) check out the field to the right.  Walk up between the upper row of
crops and the fence to find a hidden chest which contains this scroll.
4) Roll-Cut Scroll: During "To Holff Ruins" at Abyssia Forest (see ABYS1), 
follow the upper path to a large, open area.  Search the upper right corner for
a hidden chest which contains this scroll.

After you have obtained any of the scrolls, return to the soldier with them,
and he will teach the new abilities to Marco.  Learn all four abilities to
complete this sidequest.

051 - Raynie the Active (sidequest)
***********************
During the "A New Mission" node at Alistel, on the right side of the first
floor are two doors.  Enter the left door and talk to the blue soldier to
receive this sidequest.  You need to find four scrolls for Raynie.

1) Darkshield Scroll: During "The Valkyrie" node at the Sand Fortress (see
SAND3), head up to the third floor.  Walk around the right side, up the stairs,
and through the door.  Search the right side of the large room for a hidden
chest which contains this scroll.
2) Slumbering Scroll: Reward from "Second Opinion" sidequest (see 120).
3) Poisoner's Scroll: This can be found as early as the "Destroy the Ballistas"
node in Standard Chapter 5 at the Itolia Wasteland (see ITOL1).  From the
southern entrance, head north one screen, east one, and north two.  Search the
upper wall for a hidden chest which contains this scroll.
4) Piercing Scroll: Reward from "The Good Thief's Way" sidequest (see 190).

After you have obtained any of the scrolls, return to the soldier with them,
and he will teach the new abilities to Raynie.  Learn all four abilities to
complete this sidequest.

********************
During the "A New Mission" node at Alistel, head up to the castle and through
the right door.  Enter the laboratory to the left and talk to the woman, named

you will have to talk to Sonja and Raul, then come back down to talk to Sonja
again.  Return to "The Beginning" node, talk to Rosch on the first floor, and
choose to "Send Rosch".  Talk to Meese to complete this sidequest and receive
the P.Thauma Spear (ATK+23/DEF+2).  This sidequest is also a prerequisite for
unlocking the "Iron-Armed General" part of the true ending.

give it to the questgiver. Continue to the "A New Mission" node and talk to her

055 - A Letter to Tomorrow (sidequest)
**************************

room, and talk to Viola.  Continue to "Alistel's Offensive" node and talk to
Viola again.  Return to Cedric and choose to "Show journal" to complete this
sidequest.  This sidequest is also a prerequisite for unlocking the
"Valkyrie in Repose" part of the true ending.

177 - At Journey's End (sidequest)
**********************
This one is very tricky, as it has prerequisite choices to even access the
sidequest, and the game doesn't tell you.  There are three decisions you have
to make over the course of the game.

1) During the "Breakthrough" node at Gran Plain (see GRAN1), head north one
screen and west to the checkpoint.  You will hear someone in distress back
east.  Make sure to choose "Let's go save her now."
2) After the "Wolf in the Fold" node (see GRAN3), return to "Alistel's
Offensive" and talk to Viola.  Return to the "A Meeting Too Late" node and
proceed with the plot normally.  Make sure to choose "I leave it to you."
3) During the "Commander's Fall" node at the Sand Fortress (SAND6), defeat the
Thaumaton boss at the end.  Afterwards, make sure to choose "Bring back now."

Despite that last decision occuring AFTER the time when you can access the
sidequest, it is indeed a prerequisite.  Return to the "Gafka & the Scrolls"
node and head to Skalla.  Talk to Marco by the item shop.  Then, talk to Raynie
by the inn and choose "I can't give up."  Talk to Marco again to complete this
sidequest and receive the Priere Bonheur (ATK+15/MAG+32/Fear Res+5).  This
sidequest is a prerequisite for unlocking the "A Warrior in Love" part of the
true ending.
