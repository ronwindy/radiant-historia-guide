[Daydream node]
After receiving your first mission, let's promptly ignore it and go treasure
hunting first.  To your left, you should find a chest which contains 2 SOOTHING
BALMS.  Take the central elevator up to the third floor and open the chest to
the right which contains a CHARM (ATK+1/MAG+1/HP+3, equip on Raynie).  Now,
head down to the first floor.  In the upper right room are two chests which
contain a HOLY WATER and 2 HERBAL TEAS.

In the Second Ward, there is a tavern to the left and the equipment shop to the
right.  Climb both stairs on the right and run down to a chest which contains
an HERBAL TEA.  Return to the main corridor and head down to the First Ward.

Climb the stairs to your right and follow the path around to a chest which
contains 300 G.  Go back to the corridor.  To the left is the item shop and the
inn is to the right.  Check out the inn's second floor where you can find a
chest which contains BEAST SKINS (DEF+9/MDF+12, equip on Stocke).

With the new money you found, return to the equipment shop and purchase a
Custom Sword for Stocke.  Hand down his old Bronze Sword to Marco and sell your
excess equipment.  As you leave the city, talk to Rosch, and continue to
Lazvil Hills.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game:
Stocke: Custom Sword, Beast Skins
Raynie: Spear, Brigandine, Charm
Marco: Bronze Sword, Brigandine

Immediately upon your arrival, you are thrown into some tutorial battles.  The
instruction manual and in-game tutorials go over this well enough, so I don't
feel the need to do so again.  When you regain control of Stocke, head east to
the Woodland Path.  Below you, is a chest which contains 3 SOOTHING BALMS.
Continue east to another scene.

Once again, promptly ignore where you are told to go, and head down to a chest
which contains 3 ROYAL JELLIES.  Now, head north and complete another tutorial
battle.  Proceed north to Highway West and you will gain the Strike ability.
Use it to stun enemies on the field, so you can gain a preemptive strike.
Remember the crates in the cave you just passed for later and proceed east to
the Highway.  You cannot reach the chest behind the brush above, so cross the
bridge to the east.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Palomides, Granorg Archer x3
WEAKNESS: HA!
STRATEGY: None.  This is a scripted battle that you cannot win.  Stocke might
as well have just stayed in bed this morning.
*******************************************************************************

After some events, you will meet Lippti and Teo of Historia.  When you regain
control of Stocke, first head south to the blockade.  Lippti and Teo will
appear again to give you superhuman strength to move them.  Again, ignore the
blockade and head north to the Highway West.  Enter the cave and move the
crates to gain access to a chest which contains a BRONZE MAIL (DEF+14/MDF+10,
equip on Marco).  Return to the blockade, follow the path to another battle,
and you will make it out alive this time.

[Awakening node]
Upon awakening back at Alistel, leave the infirmary and try to leave to the
right.  Afterwards, you will be given a critical decision to make.

[The Beginning node]
Head upstairs and talk to Rosch.  Now, you have a decision to make: to Join
the Rosch Brigade or to Stay under Heiss.  This is where the main timeline will
split into Standard History (SH) by staying under Heiss and Alternate History
(AH) by joining the Rosch Brigade.  Eventually, you will need to use the White
Chronicle to take both paths, so don't worry about which path to take first.
You're not going to be locked out of anything.  I would recommend joining the
Rosch Brigade first, since it unlocks some sidequests.  If you wish to proceed
by staying under Heiss, then skip to ALST3.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game:
Stocke: Custom Sword, Beast Skins
Raynie: Spear, Brigandine, Charm
Marco: Bronze Sword, Bronze Mail

03) Alistel (Alternate Prologue) [ALST2]
==============================================================================

ALISTEL (Alternate Prologue) [ALST2]
Recommended Level: 4
Recommended Party: Stocke, Raynie, Marco
Enemies: None
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
[Joining Rosch Brigade node]
After talking with Rosch, Raul, and Kiel, Rosch will join your party.  Since
you can only use three party members at a time, use the Party command in the
menu screen to switch in Rosch for Marco.  This will leave you with a mage to
use, while keeping Marco's MP up for healing outside of battle.  Also, give
Marco's Bronze Mail to Raynie, while he is inactive.  You should also switch
Raynie's stronger Spear with Rosch's Light Lance, since he'll be using physical
attacks far more often.

Before heading to your next mission, you can pick up your first sidequests now.
Take the elevator down to the first floor and talk to the receptionist behind
the desk to the left to receive the [Soldier Recruit] sidequest.  Head down to
the First Ward and talk to the soldier on the left to receive the [Night Watch]
sidequest.  You will eventually need to buy two Anti-Sleeps for him, but
they're not available right now.

Check out the inn and talk to the two mercenaries there to advance the
[Soldier Recruit] sidequest.  Return to the receptionist to complete it and
receive 1500 G for your reward.  Instead of upgrading your equipment here, I
would save it for now, as better equipment will become available in the very
near future.  Leave Alistel and head for Lazvil Hills again.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game:
Stocke: Custom Sword, Beast Skins
Raynie: Light Lance, Bronze Mail, Charm
Marco: Bronze Sword, Brigandine
Rosch: Spear, Brigandine

Upon arriving at Alma Mine, you have your second decision to make.  This time,
there is a right and wrong answer.  If you want to unlock every node in the
game, you will want to choose the wrong answer, as this unlocks bad endings
throughout the game.  Choosing to "Wait in ambush" will unlock
[The Fallen Capital] ending.  Otherwise, choosing to "Send scouts" will prompt
a roadblock in the story that can only be overcome in the other timeline.

[The Closed Mine node]
If you chose to wait in ambush, then you will be sent back to the beginning of
the chapter, so press Start to skip the cutscene.  Fortunately, since this was
a critical decision point, a travel node has been created, so you can just skip
back to that decision.  At a save point, choose "Go to Historia", return to
"The Closed Mine" node, and choose to "Send Scouts".  While you won't get a bad
ending this time, the plot cannot proceed on this timeline for now.  If you
have already resolved this roadblock, then skip to ALMA2.

[Breaking Rocks node]
Before checking out the other timeline, there's still things you can do here.
Walk down to find a merchant outside of a house.  Buy 3 Horn Charms for Raynie
to receive a good boost to her Magic stat that will last a very long time.  I
would recommend saving your remaining funds for better purchases later.  Inside
the house is a chest which contains 3 SOOTHING BALMS.  Go up the ramp to the
left to find a chest which contains 200 G.

Since you are unable to continue further, use Historia to return to "The
Beginning" node.  This time, talk to Rosch and tell him you are choosing to
"Stay under Heiss".

Feepos can randomly drop a Vital Sword, which would be really nice for Marco.
You cannot procure any of the treasures yet.  Go west to the Highway and down
the ramp to revise your destination.  Walk south to the Woodland Path East,
west around the river, and north to the other side of the river.  Choosing to
"Go to Alma Mine" will unlock [The Queen Ascendant] ending.

[The Messenger's News node]
If you choose to stay with Heiss first, you will need to return to "The
Beginning" node and choose to Join the Rosch Brigade.  If you need to do that,
then skip to ALST2.  Otherwise, return to "The Messenger's News" node, and
choose to "Wait for Informant".

[No-Show node]
Go south one screen and west to the Woodland Path.  Along the way, you will
hear something.

[Echoing Hills node]
Heal everyone up and continue north to find out what's making all the ruckus.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Desert Pirate Hertz, Red Sand Bram
WEAKNESS: None
STRATEGY: Ideally, you want everyone to take their turns in one round.  Use
Change, as needed.  Have Marco Grapple Hertz, Raynie cast Thunder, and Stocke
use Push Assault to reduce their damage output.  In subsequent rounds, Stocke
should finish the combo with Power Wave.  Only heal if your HP drops below 100.
*******************************************************************************

For defeating them, you will have saved the merchant Tarquin.  In return, he
will teach you the Bomber Skill.  This will also allow you to proceed back at
Alma Mine in Alternate History.  If you want to do so, then skip to ALMA2.

[Struggle with Bandits node]
Before proceeding with your mission, let's put that new bomber skill to use.
Remember all those barrels we've seen along the way?  Apparently, people just
leave them full of explosives in this world.  Go back east to the next screen
and north until you see some barrels on the right.  Push one of them around to
the rocks on the left.  Press L to prime the explosive, back away, and press L
again to detonate them and blow away the rocks.  Behind them is a chest which
contains a HOLY WATER.

Return north to where you met the spy and enter the cave.  Sadly, the soldier
trapped there has died.  Fortunately, you can use the barrel to gain access to
the chest next to him which contains a BREEZE ARMLET (SPD+1, equip on Stocke).
Examine the corpse to receive the [Accident in the Cave] sidequest.  If only
we could have saved him before this occurred.  It would be rather inconvenient
to do so right now, and he's not going anywhere for awhile.

There is yet another barrel at the entrance by the right side of Highway East.
Push it down and blow away the rocks to reach the Southern Path.  Behind the
tree on the right is a chest which contains CELESTIAL TEA.  You may want to
return to Alistel to rest up at the inn.  Otherwise, return to the Woodland
Path, go west and south to reach Judgement Cliff.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game:
Stocke: Custom Sword, Beast Skins, Charm, Breeze Armlet

[Breakthrough node]
There is no treasure to be found here for now, so it's very straight-forward.
In the Sand Fortress South area, hug the west cliffs to avoid stepping on any
traps on the main road.  Before entering the fortress, you will also find a
merchant.  I would recommend buying 6 Fang Charms, giving 3 of each to Stocke
and Marco.  A Rune Spear will greatly boost Raynie's magic stat.  All of these
will last you for a very long time.  However, I'd save the remaining money for
buying armor later.  You can also buy 2 Anti-Sleeps for the [Night Watch]
sidequest, which we'll be completing soon enough.  If you can afford it, I
would also purchase a Rune Edge for Stocke.  Maybe try buying it after looting
some of the soldiers.  Hand down his old Custom Sword to Marco

The path through the Sand Fortress can be very short.  If you just walk right
up the middle staircases, you can make it up to the third floor without a
fight.  Then, walk around the to the right and up the stairs to the exit.
However, I'd strongly recommend fighting every soldier that you can, just for
the gold and experience.  Plus, you still might pick up a Vital Blade from the
Feepos around here too.  When you're ready, go through the door atop the third
floor for your last trial.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Granorg Defender, Granorg Archer x2, Light Granorgite x2
WEAKNESS: None
STRATEGY: Marco should cast Magic Boost on Raynie and Stocke, while they use
Push Assault and Thunder to take out a Light Granorgite and Granorg Archer.
Once they're all out of the way, I would use the Change command to string
together six consecutive turns of alternating between physical attacks and
spells to finish off the Granorg Defender.
******************************************************************************

For defeating them, you will gain access to the Sand Fortress North area.  The
exit can be found to the northeast.  Before continuing on to the Gran Plain,
you should rest up back at Alistel.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game:
Stocke: Rune Edge, Beast Skins, Fang Charm x3
Raynie: Rune Spear, Brigandine, Horn Charm x3
