57) Sand Fortress (Alternate Chapter 5) [SAND6]
==============================================================================

SAND FORTRESS (Alternate Chapter 5) [SAND6]
Recommended Level: 56
Recommended Party: Stocke, Gafka, Rosch
Enemies: Alistel Soldier, Auto Defender, Prod. Thaumachine (Lightning)
=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
[Commander's Fall node]
I'd recommend fighting every enemy you can around here to get some much-needed
experience for Rosch & Gafka.  If necessary, Rosch's Cross Spike can be useful
for taking out those Auto Defenders.  Otherwise, just head straight up the
middle to the third floor.  Then, walk around the right side and up the stairs.
Bring in Aht to replace Gafka and continue up to meet the commander.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Cannon x2, Thaumaton
WEAKNESS: Fire, Lightning
STRATEGY: Use a Magic Herb on Stocke and have him cast Heat Blade to take out
the two Cannons.  Then, use a Magic Herb on Aht, lay out a couple Volt Star
Traps, and Grapple the Thaumaton onto them for a quick victory.
*******************************************************************************

After the battle, Bergas will arrive to prompt you for a decision on your next
move.  Make sure to choose "Bring back now", as this is a requirement to
unlocking the [At Journey's End] sidequest.  Either way, the plot will proceed
the same way.

[Forbidden Weaponry node]
Talk to Rosch and join the meeting.  After some dialogue, you will be prompted
for a decision.  Choosing to "Accept the truce" will unlock the
[Divine Judgement] ending.

[Etherion node]
Before moving on, return to the "Gafka & the Scrolls" node and head to Skalla.
Talk to Marco by the item shop.  If you have been following the walkthrough,
you will finally receive the [At Journey's End] sidequest.  If not, search for
"At Journey's End" in the walkthrough to find the three prerequisites you
need to make.  Oddly enough, one of the choices occurs AFTER this point in the
plot, yet affects it retroactively.

Talk to Raynie by the inn to be prompted for a decision.  Choosing "I'll stop
fighting" will unlock the [Oncoming Calamity] ending.  Return to the same
decision point and choose "I can't give up" to advance the [At Journey's End]
sidequest.  Talk to Marco again to complete the [At Journey's End] sidequest
and receive the PRIERE BONHEUR (ATK+15/MAG+52/Fear Res+5, equip on Raynie).

When you are ready, return to the "Etherion" node and choose to "Reject the
truce."  However, someone else has different ideas.

*******************************************************************************
BOSS TIME: Shadow x3
WEAKNESS: Poison
STRATEGY: Start by using Right Assault on the Shadow in the back row.  Then,
use the Change command to string together five consecutive turns.  Lay any Star
Trap in the lower left corner.  Finally, from the lower right Shadow, use Right
Assault, Push Assault, Left Assault, and Left Assault to defeat them all, using
Eruca's Trans-turn, as needed.
*******************************************************************************

[Royal Talisman node]
[A Black Disciple node]
After surviving the battle, everyone will discuss the assault on Granorg.

Recommended Equipment at this point in the game
Stocke: Pieti Edge, Moirae Manteau, Crystal Charm x3
Raynie: Priere Bonheur, Tungsten Mail, Crystal Charm x3
Marco: Gaia Blade, Tungsten Mail, Rook Stud x3
