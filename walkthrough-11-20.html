Gran Plain SE

There's no treasure here either, so just head north to the Gran Plain East, and west to the checkpoint. Yet again, you'll be distracted by someone in distress back east.

📜 Sidequest Prerequisite Trigger

Make sure to choose "Let's go save her now." as this is a strict requirement to unlocking the [At Journey's End] sidequest later in the campaign. Either way, you'll have to deal with the disturbance immediately.

⚔️ Boss: Sabertooth Tiger x2
Encounter
Elemental Weakness: None
Have Marco cast Magic Boost on Raynie, while Stocke and Raynie use Push Assault to shove the tigers into the back row. Then, use two combos of Left Assault, any elemental spell, and a physical strike from Marco to finish them off quickly.

For defeating them, you will meet Aht, Liese, and Vanoss, all Beastkind of the Satyros tribe. Aht will now join your party! I recommend handing Stocke's Beast Skins down to Aht, as we're going to purchase superior armor for him very soon.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Beastkind]

Saved the young Satyros shaman Aht on the eastern plains. She joins Stocke's company with her guardians Liese and Vanoss.

⚠️ Temporal Choice Point — Bad Ending Warning

Now, return west to the checkpoint where you have a critical decision to make.
Choosing to "Force your way through" will unlock the [A Sealed Path] Possible History (Bad Ending).

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoints: [Stocke's Display] & [Sword Dancing]

Return to [Stocke's Display] node. Trying to "Abandon the idea" will only lead to a dead end for now until we learn proper sword-dancing techniques.

📜 Sidequest Round-Up & Timeline Detour

There's nothing more we can do here for now. If you have already resolved this roadblock, skip to GRAN2 (#17). Otherwise, this is the perfect time to wrap up several high-value sidequests across time:

  1. Return to "The Beginning" node and choose to Join the Rosch Brigade. If you have at least 5,300 G, purchase a Custom Lance for Rosch.
  2. Head down to the First Ward and deliver the two Anti-Sleeps purchased earlier to the soldier on the left to complete [Night Watch] → Reward: Alistel Edge (ATK+16 / MAG+6, equip on Marco).
  3. Continue the plot and return to the cave-in site (west and south one screen, hug river clockwise north to the spy location). In this timeline, the soldier is still alive: use the explosive barrel to blast the rocks. Talk to the soldier to complete [Accident in the Cave] → Reward: Right-Cut Scroll (for Marco, usable later).
  4. Either walk to Alma Mine or use Historia to skip ahead to [The Closed Mine] node.

If you have resolved saving the merchant in Alternate History, you can continue with the plot here. Choose to "Send Scouts" and you will gain full access to the Alma Mine interior.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Duel in the Dark]

Scouted ahead into the depths of Alma Mine to flank Granorg occupation forces.

Before entering the mine depths, step outside and talk to the merchant near the house. Purchase 3 Silver Mails for Stocke, Raynie, and Rosch. Also buy 3 Pawn Studs for Marco, as his utility skills don't rely heavily on base strength or magic.

Take the left path until you find explosive barrels. Pull one all the way around to the right path to blow away the boulders blocking a chest containing HEAVY MAIL (DEF+21 / MDF+15 / SPD-2; equip on Marco immediately). Don't worry about the minor speed reduction—physical defense is king in this game! Return to the barrels and blow up the nearby boulders to access the northern shaft.

Go right to the fork, up, and left to find another barrel. Pull it right to blast the obstruction. After the cutscene, head left to a side room with a chest holding 800 G. Return to the blasted path and advance to the next level.

Take either path around to the save point. Head left to find two barrels. Pull one barrel back carefully so you don't detonate both simultaneously—you need both to clear the rubble. To the left is a chest with 4 ROYAL JELLIES. Clear the boulders, pull the second barrel right, and follow the eastern corridor to a chest with a HEALING HERB.

Heal up at the save point and prep your party. If you are fielding Rosch over Raynie, pass Raynie's Horn Charms to Stocke. Head north to engage the enemy commanders.

⚔️ Boss: Granorg Archer x3, Light Granorgite x4
Encounter
Elemental Weakness: None
You will definitely want Marco for this battle. Start by casting Magic Boost on your primary offensive caster. Any combination of Push/Left Assaults stacked with area spells will wipe them out in bundles. Marco's Guard Boost is also excellent for mitigating damage from so many concurrent enemy turns.

After the battle, the Rosch Brigade successfully secures the mine and returns triumphantly to Alistel.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [A New Mission]

Regained control of Stocke outside the Alistel tavern following promotion in the Rosch Brigade.

Climb the stairs to the left and talk to Cedric at the bottom to receive [A Letter to Tomorrow]. Now that the First Ward road is repaired, access the upper left platform to loot two chests containing a SEED RING (sell for gold) and 1,500 G. Head up to the castle.

📜 Massive Castle Sidequest Hub
  • Talk to the castle receptionist on the left to receive [Engineer Recruit].
  • Talk to the woman tending flowers on the lower-right of 1F to receive [A Flower of Promise].
  • Enter the room above her and speak with the new blue soldier to receive [Raynie the Active] and [Marco the Calm].
  • Learn Skill: If you completed Accident in the Cave, talk to the soldier again and choose "Teach it to Marco" to consume the Right-Cut Scroll — Marco permanently learns Right Assault!
  • Enter the Laboratory to the left of the infirmary and speak with Meese to trigger [Red-Letter Day]. Finally, speak with Sonja in the infirmary.
⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Summoned by Raul]

Descend to 1F and talk to Sonja to advance [Red-Letter Day].

⚠️ Temporal Choice Point — Bad Ending Warning

Jump back to "The Beginning" node and talk to Rosch on 1F.
Choosing to "Chase after" Sonja will unlock the [A Knight's End] Possible History (Bad Ending).
Choosing to "Send Rosch" will correctly advance the [Red-Letter Day] sidequest!

Before turning in quests, warp to "The Closed Mine" node. Enter the house and speak with engineer Berman to advance [Engineer Recruit]. Return to [A New Mission] node (press Start to skip dialogue cutscenes).

Speak with the castle receptionist to complete [Engineer Recruit] → Reward: 3,000 G. Then talk to Meese in the lab to complete [Red-Letter Day] → Reward: P.Thauma Spear (ATK+23 / DEF+2; equip on Rosch).

Take the elevator up to 3F and report to Lt. General Raul in his office on the right.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Through Lazvil]

Received marching orders from Raul to advance south through Lazvil Hills toward the Sand Fortress.

⚔️ Boss: Shadow
Encounter
Elemental Weakness: None
Shadow immediately casts Heal Zone on his opening turn. Whenever an enemy places a regeneration zone, shove them off it instantly! Have Marco cast Magic Boost on Raynie, followed by Raynie casting an offensive spell and Stocke using Push Assault to shove Shadow into the back row away from the zone. Then, cast Magic Boost on Stocke and blast Shadow with consecutive spells to burn him down.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [The Valkyrie]

Reported to Field Marshal Viola in the central command room on 1F.

First, step outside and purchase two Knight Studs from the merchant for Marco. Inside, head up the central staircase to 2F. In the upper-left room, you can sleep in the bunk beds for a free full HP/MP recovery.

⚔️ Crucial Ability Acquisition: Sword-Dancing & Field Strike Upgrade

Outside the 2F bunk room, talk to Kiel training on the left terrace. He will teach Stocke the art of sword-dancing, permanently upgrading your Strike ability to chop down thick tall grass and brush across all timelines!

Head up to 3F and exit through the northern archway to Sand Fortress North. In the upper-left corner is a patch of tall grass: use your new sword-dancing strike to clear it and open two chests containing 2 HERBAL TEAS and the formidable WISP BLADE (ATK+18 / MAG+12 / HP+50; equip on Marco).

Return to 1F and speak with Viola in the command room to advance [A Letter to Tomorrow]. Then speak with Rosch to deploy the brigade to the defensive perimeter.

⚠️ Temporal Choice Point — Bad Ending Warning

As the enemy battalion breaches the gates, you must choose your tactical response.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoints: [Conditions Worsen] & [Ambush Tactics]

Return to [The Valkyrie] node. Choosing "Prioritize the mission" results in a tactical dead end for now.
To resolve this military stalemate, we must travel back to [Stocke's Display] in Standard History and put our sword-dancing strike to use on the Gran Plain.

With sword-dancing learned from Kiel, return to Gran Plain. North of the save point, cut the tall grass to the east to access a chest containing a FIRST AID KIT. From the save point, head west to the checkpoint and choose "Abandon the idea" to proceed!

✨ Tactical Spotlight: Aht's Elemental Trap Mechanics

Swap Raynie out for Aht and equip her with Raynie's Horn Charms. Aht places elemental traps on empty tiles: when Stocke or Marco uses Push, Left, or Right Assault to shove an enemy onto that tile, it detonates for massive elemental damage! Against Goblins, have Aht cast Polaris on turn 1 to grant party-wide poison immunity.

To the northwest, pull the explosive barrel down to blast the boulders, revealing a chest with a BISHOP STUD (DEF+6 / MDF+6; equip on Marco). Continue west into Gran Plain West.

Cut the tall grass to the west to open a chest containing a HOLY WATER. Head south into the Forest Clearing. To the left of the save point is a chest with 3 ANTI-POISONS. Advance south to establish camp with Vanoss and Liese.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Collecting Wood]

Aht teaches Stocke the legendary Mana Sight ability, allowing him to perceive invisible objects, hidden treasure chests, and explosive traps throughout the world!

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Preparing a Meal]

Gather the 5 hidden Withered Branches across the plains using Mana Sight:

  1. Branch 1: Just north of the Forest Clearing campsite.
  2. Branch 2: Gran Plain West in the upper-right corner behind tall grass.
  3. Branch 3: Central Gran Plain area along the northern path.
  4. Branch 4: Gran Plain Northwest along the eastern cliff wall.
  5. Branch 5: Walk south two screens to South Hill. Switch Raynie back in and give her the Horn Charms before searching by the tree on the right!
⚔️ Boss: Fumble & Rare Mushroom
Encounter
Elemental Weakness: Poison, Fire
Finally, a boss with clear elemental weaknesses! Open with Marco's Push Assault on Fumble, Stocke's Fire to incinerate the mushroom, and Poison Break on Fumble. On Round 2, have Marco cast Magic Boost on Raynie, Stocke use Right Assault to group the foes, and Raynie blast both with Fire. Use the Change command to string together 6 consecutive turns alternating physical strikes and fire spells before Fumble can summon reinforcements.

Victory yields Fresh Meat for dinner. Return to the Forest Clearing and speak with Vanoss/Liese to sleep. During the night, Aht entrusts Stocke with the White Page.

🌟 Mana Burst Awakening: Turn Break

Visit Historia via the save point: Lippti and Teo channel the White Page to unlock your very first Mana Burst: Turn Break! Turn Break completely deletes an enemy's upcoming turn panel without consuming your own active turn.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Aht's Prayer]

March north through Gran Plain NW to trigger a cutscene at the border. Exit north to arrive at the gates of Granorg.
Pro-Tip: Maximize at least one party member's Mana Burst gauge before entering for an upcoming sidequest!

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [An Ancient City]

Entered the grand capital of Queen Protea to establish contact with Specint undercover agents.

Your contact is located in the Commercial District to the east, but explore the Downtown area to the west first. Climb the stairs to the Downtown inn and rest up. In one of the 2F guest rooms, loot a chest containing an ANTI-PARALYTIC.

Climb the stairs next to the item shop and visit the equipment vendor. Buy two Heavy Mails for Stocke and Raynie. Finally, speak with the cloaked man standing between the two central staircases.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Duty Revealed]

After receiving mission briefings, head west to Main Street. Speak to the guard by the save point twice to receive [Mankind & Beastkind].

Visit the Downtown inn, head to 2F, and talk to Kaizan in the right room to receive [One & Only]. Exit the inn, walk around its left side, and climb the stairs to find Liese. Talk to her to advance [Mankind & Beastkind], then report back to the guard.

Head west two screens to the Back Alley. Enter the tavern and talk to the bartender for clues regarding the palace entrance.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Castle Sewers]

Before plunging into the sewers, embark on a multi-timeline Mana Sight treasure tour across Vainqueur!

🔍 The Grand Mana Sight World Tour
  1. Alma Mine: Jump to "The Closed Mine" node. Talk to the miner inside the house to advance [One & Only]. Use Mana Sight around the house to reveal a hidden chest containing the IMPALE CORE (for Rosch). Climb the ramp to the right to reveal a hidden chest with 2 MANA CRYSTALS.
  2. Alistel Castle: Return to "The Beginning" node and choose to Join the Rosch Brigade:
    • 3F Hugo's office: Hidden chest with 2,000 G.
    • 3F Raul's office: Hidden chest with a PANACEA PLUS.
    • 2F Viola's office: Hidden chest with a SHIELD SEED.
    • 2F Heiss' office: Hidden chest containing the RHAPSODY (ATK+12 / MAG+18 / SPD+5, save for Aht).
    • Second Ward upper right platform: Hidden chest with 2,000 G.
    • First Ward alley behind item shop: Hidden chest containing the FIRE PACT (Stocke's skill scroll).
    • First Ward upper right platform: Hidden chest with 2 MANA CRYSTALS.
    • Inn 2F: Talk to Kaizan to advance [One & Only].
  3. Lazvil Hills: Travel to Lazvil Hills:
    • Use the barrel to blow up the southern rocks. Cut the tall grass on the left for 2 HEALING HERBS.
    • Search the left path with Mana Sight for a hidden chest containing a RUNE CLAW (ATK+41 / MDF+5).
    • Detonate the hidden barrel on the right to reach Seaside Road, looting 2 HERBAL TEAS at the bottom.
    • Save at the Woodland Path S-point before confronting the massive beast on the central highway.
⚔️ Boss: Giant Boar
Field Hunt
Elemental Weakness: Fire
The Giant Boar starts by casting Kiai Charge to prepare a lethal strike. String together 8–9 consecutive turns using either the Change command or your new Mana Burst: Turn Break (which deletes the Boar's action without consuming your own turn!). Once chained, cast Magic Boost on Stocke and Raynie, then unload 6 consecutive Fire spells to obliterate him!
⚠️ Critical Sidequest Warning — The Ultimate Dish

Defeating the Giant Boar rewards you with SAVORY BOAR MEAT.
DO NOT talk to Hilster about [The Ultimate Dish] yet! Doing so prematurely will cause you to lose your Savory Boar Meat and force you to repeat the entire hunt!

Loot remaining hidden chests in Lazvil Hills: SUPERIOR TEA (upper highway wall), PANACEA (west area tall grass), and 3 PANACEAS (northeast fork tall grass).

Warp back to "Castle Sewers" node. Talk to Kaizan at the Granorg inn, then in Alistel, and finally back in Granorg to complete [One & Only] → Reward: FIRESTAR PACT (for Aht).

Rest up at the inn and search behind the tavern in the Back Alley to reveal the hidden entrance into the palace sewers.

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoint: [Secret of the Sewers]

Infiltrated the Granorg palace drainage system. Watch out for ceiling ambush drops (*) and operate wall levers (L) to lower corresponding drawbridges (D).

From the entrance, follow the corridor up one screen and take either path right. Walk down to find a lever lowering the drawbridges. Follow the left wall around to a wide chamber. Take the lower left path to find a chest holding 2 HERBAL TEAS.

Return to the open area and take the upper right path. Flip the second lever to lower adjacent drawbridges. Follow the lower right path to a chest containing 2 SPECIAL TEAS. Walk down the rightmost lower path to trigger another bridge lever.

From the central hub, take the left lower path. Fork left to open a chest containing a MAIL VEST (DEF+22 / MDF+17; equip on Stocke and pass his Heavy Mail to Rosch). Fork down and left for 2,000 G.

👧 Civilian Escort — Claire

Head right to discover a lost girl named Claire. Escorting her out of the waterway unlocks the [Escort] Historia node and rewards you with the SMALL SATCHEL and a HOLY WATER. On the two adjacent paths to the right, loot chests with a WIND ARMLET (SPD+3) and 2 PANACEAS.

Return inside and retrace your steps to the eastern corridor. Head up the right path to loot 2 MANA CRYSTALS, and the left path for a MEDICLOTH.

Advance up, left, and north to the final open chamber. Pull the lever to drop the final drawbridge. Take the lower right path for 2,000 G, then climb the ladder on the upper right path to emerge inside Granorg Palace!

⚠️ Temporal Choice Point — Bad Ending Warning

In the Underground Passage, you will be prompted for a critical decision.
Choosing "I'll be the decoy" will unlock the [Fated Assassination] Possible History (Bad Ending).

⏳ Historia Node Checkpoints: [How to Disappear] & [High Alert]

To bypass the palace lockdown, we must warp back to [The Valkyrie] node in Alternate History and utilize our newly acquired Mana Sight ability at the Sand Fortress!
