⚔️ 3x3 Grid Battle Mechanics

Grid manipulation, enemy stacking, and combo chains
Left Center Right

B
C
Enemy Position: 3 enemies scattered on grid

1. Grid Row Modifiers

Enemies in the Front Row deal 100% physical damage but take 100% damage. Enemies in the Back Row deal only 60% physical damage and take 60% damage. Push high-threat melee enemies to the back row to survive!

2. Enemy Stacking & Area Nukes

Use movement skills (Push Assault, Left/Right Assault, Grapple) to shove multiple enemies onto the exact same grid tile. Once stacked, any single-target spell (e.g. Fire, G-Frost, Thunder) hits all stacked enemies simultaneously for massive combo multiplier bonuses!

3. Turn Swapping ("Change" Command)

Stocke and allies can use the Change command to trade turn slots with any enemy or party member. This lets you align all 3 party members consecutively to unleash 10+ hit combo chains before enemies can react.

🏰 The Nations of Vainqueur

Geopolitics, Mana decline, and desertification

Alistel

Leader: Prophet Noah & General Hugo

A technologically advanced mountain nation powered by Thaumatech mana engines. Threatened by sand encroachment, Alistel wages a desperate religious war against Granorg.

Granorg

Leader: Queen Protea (Tyrant)

An ancient kingdom blessed with fertile land, now languishing under the ruthless tyranny of Queen Protea after the assassination of King Victor.

Celestia

Leader: Patriarch Barranca

A secluded woodland haven protected by powerful sacred barriers, inhabited by the goat-horned Satyros beast tribe who revere Mana and trees.

Forgia

Leader: Elder Galva

The rugged mountainous homeland of the proud, powerhouse Gutral beast tribe, renowned for unyielding physical might and deep honor.

Cygnus

Leader: King Garland (Desert Gladiator)

A bustling desert haven and free oasis city-state that welcomes wandering mercenaries, outlaws, and traders escaping the war.

Sand Fortress

Strategic Chokepoint

A massive three-story military barrier guarding the border pass between Gran Plain and Judgement Cliff. The site of brutal sieges throughout both timelines.
