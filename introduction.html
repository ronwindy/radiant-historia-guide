📜 I. Introduction & Guide Philosophy

Author foreword by Henri "HC" Bailly
"This is a complete walkthrough for Radiant Historia, one of the best RPGs that I have played in years. If you want to play an RPG with awesome music, an epic plot, and simple, yet engaging battle mechanics, this is the game for you."

I hope this walkthrough will help you have more fun with the game and provide information organized as efficiently as possible. Below are the key design rules and methodologies applied throughout every chapter of this compendium:

🔥 100% Weaknesses & Treasures

Includes the elemental weaknesses of every monster encounter, as well as every single treasure in the game that is currently cataloged. You will never miss a hidden chest or mana stone node.

📈 No Grinding Necessary

The recommended levels in this guide are calibrated for the main character (Stocke) based on fighting every random encounter once naturally along the path. Even if your levels are well below mine, don't worry about it!

🗡️ Smart Stealing & Drop Rules

I avoid repetitive farming of gold or random rare equipment drops. The sole exception is stealing equipment, since enemy steals are guaranteed to yield the target item without wasting hours of grinding.

⏳ Spoiler Policy & Node Completion Warning

I will do my best to avoid spoilers to the plot in the walkthrough. That said, character names, dungeon locations, and mission objectives are necessary to guide your travel. Be warned that Section III (Node Completion) contains unavoidable plot spoilers if read ahead of time.

💎 Boss Equipment Farming Clarification

While certain end-game bosses and rare enemies drop "ultimate" gear that can be fought repeatedly through timeline jumps, this guide does not require repetitive boss farming—it takes too much time for not enough reward to beat the story.
